🎯 趣味引入
「断线的故事」

📖 读一读下面这段话：

Dear Maria,

Today I'm in Venice. I chose this postcard. I wanted you to see how beautiful this place is!

I first arrived. I couldn't believe my eyes! The canals, the bridges, the old buildings — everything was amazing.

I had pizza for lunch. It was the best pizza I've ever had. It was made by a chef who has been cooking for 40 years.

Love, Suyi

❓ 找出至少 3 处需要加 connectives 的地方：

① "I chose this postcard" 后面加 because（Reason 原因 — 为什么选这张明信片？）
② "I first arrived" 前面加 When（Time 时间 — 什么时候发生的事情？）
③ "It was made by a chef..." 前面加 because（Reason 原因 — 为什么是最好的披萨？）

📌 技巧：先判断逻辑关系（时间 / 原因 / 结果），再选词。

🎯 趣味引入

🔍 Step 1: 读全文，判断逻辑关系

📖 核心知识
CONNECTIVES — Your Writing Glue

① Time 时间

when, then, next, afterwards, later

② Reason 原因

because, as, since

③ Result 结果

so, therefore, as a result, consequently

④ Concession 让步

although, but, however, nevertheless

⑤ Addition 补充

and, also, besides, furthermore, moreover

⑥ Example 举例

for example, for instance, such as

⚠️ 今天重点：①②③（Time / Reason / Result）

写作话题练习 + 答案核对

从以下三个话题任选一个，用 Time + Reason + Result connectives 写 3 句话：

1. "Describe a time when your travel plans went wrong."
→ 用 when, because, therefore

2. "Compare two hotels you've stayed at."
→ 用 however, although, whereas

3. "为什么旅行对年轻人很重要？"
→ 用 because, for example, so

✏️ 独立完成三个话题，每句话至少使用 1 个 connective

注意：不要 because 和 so 连用，也不要 although 和 but 连用

⚠️ 常见错误：

• because + so 连用（×）
• although + but 连用（×）

📖 核心知识
后三类 Connectives — 高级连接词

⑦ Condition 条件

if, unless, provided that

例句：If it rains, we will stay at home.

⑧ Purpose 目的

in order to, so that, for the purpose of

例句：She studies hard so that she can get good grades.

⑨ Clarification 澄清

in other words, that is to say, to be more specific

例句：The weather was terrible; in other words, we couldn't go out.

⚠️ 语法注意：

• in order to + V（后接动词原形）
• so that + 句子（后接完整句子）

「耳朵侦探」

👂 听以下句子，找出其中的修辞手法：

1. "The baby's bare bottom bumped the bed."

修辞手法：________

2. "The wind whispered through the trees."

修辞手法：________

1 → Alliteration（头韵） — 重复 /b/ 音（bare bottom bumped bed）
2 → Personification（拟人） — 风不会 whisper（低语），赋予风人的特征

🎯 概念总结
Alliteration & Personification

🔤 ALLITERATION 头韵

= same beginning sound
（同一辅音在相邻单词重复）

→ "The bare bottom bumped the bed"

Creates: rhythm, emphasis, catchy titles, vivid mood

🧑 PERSONIFICATION 拟人

= giving human qualities to things
（赋予非人事物人的特征）

→ "The wind whispered through the trees"

Creates: visual images, creative writing, lively descriptions

⚠️ Don't overuse! A little goes a long way.（少即是多）

📖 例子示范
Textbook p.215 — 原文例句

例句 1（Alliteration）：

"My parents thought I was naive, but I wanted to experience a different cuisine, a different culture and a different language."

(出自课本 p.197 — /d/ 音重复)

例句 2（Personification）：

"The bustling city assaulted my senses from all sides: the raging heat that made my shirt stick to my back…"

(出自课本 p.197 — 城市不会 assault，heat 不会 rage)

例句 1 → ALLITERATION (/d/ sound)
different cuisine, different culture, different language → Creates rhythm + emphasis

例句 2 → PERSONIFICATION
"The city assaulted my senses" — 城市不会 assault
"the raging heat" — heat 不会 rage

📌 常见 Personification 表达：

• My phone died. (手机"死"了)
• Time waits for nobody. (时间不等人)
• This place has been kind to me. (这个地方对我很"友善")

🎯 趣味引入
「日记 vs 作文」
Version A (essay tone)

"Last week, I decided to try a vegetarian diet. This decision was influenced by various factors, including health concerns and environmental awareness. The experience has been eye-opening."

Version B (diary tone)

"Dear Diary, I can't believe it's been a whole week since I gave up meat! When I first told Mum, she nearly dropped her coffee cup."

Version B 是 diary entry！
→ personal tone（个人语气，写给自己的）
→ emotions（有情感）
→ specific details（有细节）
→ Dear Diary format
→ conversational language（口语化，不正式）
→ 过去时为主（记录已发生的事）

A diary entry isn't a report — it's a conversation with yourself.

🎯 概念总结
Diary Entry 的 4 个特点

1️⃣ 写给自己看 — 不需要考虑读者（Reader: yourself）

2️⃣ 第一人称 (I, we, my) — 个人视角（1st person）

3️⃣ 过去时 — 记录已发生的事（Past tense）

4️⃣ 可以不正式 — 随意表达，口语化（Informal tone）

📌 "A diary entry shows what you did, how you felt, and what you learned."

三种 Hook 技巧：

Q2 Essay — Cage Home Experience

When my Liberal Studies teacher announced we'd be researching housing in Hong Kong, I never imagined I'd end up sleeping in a cage home for a night. But that experience changed how I see this city forever.

The cage home was shockingly small — it was roughly the size of a parking space, and it was dim and damp. Lying on that hard bed, I heard the neighbour's coughing, a baby's crying, and the street noise — the city seemed to assault my senses.

I felt a wave of guilt — I had been complaining about my room being too small, but compared to this, my room was like a palace.

The turning point came when I chatted with an elderly man who had been living there for five years. He still smiled and said, "At least we have a roof."

This experience taught me that happiness isn't determined by what you own, but by how you see what you have. Since that night, I've stopped complaining about my room — whenever I feel dissatisfied, I remember that tiny metal-framed bed.
