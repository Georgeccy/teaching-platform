Task 2A · Understanding the Question

The following excerpt appeared in an article in the Hong Kong Post: Another social media trend that has been garnering significant attention is the '10-year challenge'... Posters unsurprisingly see this as a harmless—and potentially even healthy—trend... On the other hand, there are many who view the trend with scepticism, arguing that it is another case of the shallow self-centredness of social media users.

No specific role / a member of the public / a student

Newspaper readers / the general public
3. Purpose（写作目的）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选第一项。题目要求 present your opinions and discuss whether such trends can be harmful — 即讨论该趋势的正面/负面影响。其余选项范围过宽或偏离题目焦点。

✅ Tone: logical + persuasive — LTE 需要有逻辑地论证并用说服力打动读者

Part 5 · Opening Paragraph

✍️ Task 5A: 开头段范文精读

P1 >>>>>>>> Opening paragraph

Dear editor,

I am writing in response to ______ the "10-year challenge", a social media trend in which users post old and recent photos of themselves to compare how they have changed over the years. While some people ______, I believe ______
📝 写作要点：① 第一句用 I am writing in response to... 说明写信目的并引入话题；② 第二句用 While some people..., I believe... 让步承认反方观点后抛出我方立场。这是 LTE 开头段的标准句式。

Structure & Sentence Patterns · Opening

📐 开头段结构与句式模版

第一句：说明写信目的

句式示例
I am writing in response to 话题内容I am writing in response to the recent discussion surrounding the "10-year challenge".
I am writing to express my view on 话题内容I am writing to express my view on the "10-year challenge" trend.
I would like to share my opinion regarding 话题内容I would like to share my opinion regarding the "10-year challenge".

第二句：表明立场（让步 + 立场）

句式示例
While some people claim/contend/think ..., I believe/suggest/consider that 抛出我方立场.While some people contend that it promotes self-centredness, I believe it is generally harmless.
Although critics/advocates claim that ..., I strongly believe that ...Although critics claim that it is shallow, I strongly believe that it can bring meaningful benefits.

Part 6 · Body Paragraph 1 — Benefit 1

📐 PEER 论证公式

🌟 Task 6A: 根据括号中的提示补全句子，并将句子排序，形成一个合乎逻辑的主体段。

QuestionFunction
P (Point)What is your point?表明观点
E (Explain)Why / How?展开解释（为什么 / 如何）
E (Example)How / what?举例 / 列举 / 具体化
R (Result)So what? Why it matters?结果 / 影响 / 最终判断
📌 Notes: PEER 是一个整体框架，具体的逻辑链可以有所合并或增补。如一句话中结合 explain+example；或者在完成一个 explain+example 逻辑链后，并列展开另一条 explain+example 逻辑链。

Part 9 · Body Paragraph 4 — Counter-argument & Rebuttal

🤔 反驳反方观点

📍思考9.1: 有人认为10-year challenge的缺点是"encourage self-centredness"。回顾Lesson 6学过的"反驳的两种方式"，如何从"降低重要性"的角度反驳？
影响有限：大部分人都是以总结和分享的积极心态参与，出发点不是为了炫耀和博眼球
非必须："展示自我"也不是10-year challenge这个活动的锅，而是社交媒体本身的重要属性

反驳反方的两种常用方法

方法思路
降低反方的重要性• 对方影响有限 / 对方也有问题
• 对方问题可以解决
• 非必须
与我方作对比，强调我方更重要我方好处更多 / 影响更大

Appendix · Model Answer

📝 范文：Letter to the Editor — 10-Year Challenge

Task: Write a letter to the editor presenting your opinions on the "10-year challenge". (~400 words)

Dear Editor,

I am writing in response to the recent discussion surrounding the "10-year challenge", a social media trend in which users post old and recent photos of themselves to compare how they have changed over the years. While some people see this trend as another example of the shallow self-centredness often associated with social media, I believe it is generally harmless and can even bring meaningful benefits to individuals and society.

To begin with, the 10-year challenge can bring people joy and a sense of accomplishment. In today's fast-paced world, many people are too busy to organise old photos or revisit important memories. However, the fun and creative nature of this trend encourages them to do so. By searching through old pictures, people may rediscover meaningful moments, recognise goals they have achieved and appreciate how far they have come. As a result, they may experience a sense of joy, take pride in their achievements and develop a greater sense of accomplishment.

Another benefit is that the trend can help people develop more positive attitudes towards life. When comparing their past and present selves, many people realise that meaningful changes rarely happen overnight. Behind every improvement often lies perseverance, courage and hard work. For example, some may remember how they overcame serious illnesses and regained their health over the past decade. Others may reflect on how their hard work enabled them to gain admission to their dream universities or become leading professionals in their fields. This may encourage people to face future challenges with greater optimism and determination, knowing that difficulties can be overcome through persistence.

Moreover, the 10-year challenge can create opportunities for mutual encouragement and support. By sharing their experiences online, people may receive kind words and encouragement from others, making them feel supported and understood. At the same time, their stories of growth and resilience may inspire others who are facing similar struggles. Seeing someone else's journey of recovery or perseverance may encourage people not to give up during difficult times. In this way, the trend can spread positive values among social media users.

Some people may argue that the 10-year challenge encourages people to show off and seek attention from others. However, this argument overlooks the fact that sharing personal experiences has always been part of social media by nature. Therefore, it may be unfair to describe this particular trend as shallow and self-centred. In fact, many people simply join the challenge to celebrate their growth, share happy memories and enjoy looking back on how far they have come, instead of showing off about themselves.

In conclusion, although the 10-year challenge may have some drawbacks, it is generally harmless and can bring meaningful benefits to both individuals and society. It allows people to celebrate their achievements, develop more positive attitudes towards life and encourage one another through shared experiences. Rather than dismissing every social media trend as shallow or self-centred, we should consider how such platforms can be used to spread positivity, support and hope.

Yours faithfully,
Chris Wong
