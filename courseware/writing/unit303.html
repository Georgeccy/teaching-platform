Task Analysis

📋 审题：Memo 考什么？

The following memo is written to inform the Head of English of Poetry Society's entries in a poetry writing competition. However, there are formatting errors and some sentences are redundant. Rewrite the memo in a more concise manner using the correct format.
1. Role（你的身份）——你是谁？
A member of the Poetry Society（诗歌社成员）— 需要向英语科主任汇报
2. Audience（读者）——写给谁看？
Head of English（英语科主任）— 因此语气要正式、简洁、专业
3. Purpose（写作目的）——为什么写？
To inform about the poetry competition entries（通知诗歌比赛报名事宜）— 内部沟通，只传递必要信息
4. Register（语域）——用什么语气？
✅ formal / semi-formal — 备忘录是 workplace 内部文件，语气正式或半正式，简洁不冗余

Format

📐 Memo 的标准格式

Memo = memorandum，是内部文件（internal document），只用于同一组织内沟通。

部分内容与要求
To:收件人（如：Mr. Ho）
From:发件人（如：Chris Wong）
Date:日期（如：15 May 20__）
Subject:主题（如：Poetry writing competition）
Opening直接说明目的：I would like to inform you of...
Body传递必要信息，简洁不冗余
⚠️ 注意：Memo 没有 salutation（称呼）、没有 closing remark（结尾客套）、没有 complimentary close（敬语结尾）。这是与 letter/email 最大的区别。
Letter: date → address → salutation → opening → body → closing remark → sender's name

Email: Date: → To: → From: → Re/Subject: → salutation → opening → body → closing remark → sender's name

Memo: To: → From: → Date: → Subject: → opening → body

关键区别：Memo 无 salutation、无 closing remark、无 complimentary close。

Part Three · Report Writing

📐 什么是 Report？

🤔先想一想：下面哪段话更符合报告的特点？点击你的选择！
✅ B 是正确的！报告需要使用 facts（事实）、passive voice（被动语态）、third person（第三人称），保持 objective（客观）和 impersonal（非个人化）。
📖 定义：A report presents information to readers; it should be well-organized and easy to understand. Reports can be written to present survey findings, explain a problem, or relate how an event went.

Part Three · Structure

📊 Report 的标准结构

部分功能与要求
Title给出报告主题（如：Report on factors contributing to job satisfaction）
Introduction提供背景信息；说明写作目的（如：This report aims at presenting the findings of the survey...）
Body用 headings 和 subheadings 分段；每段有 clear topic sentence + supporting sentences + connectives；只呈现 facts，不呈现 opinions
Conclusion总结 Body 的主要发现
Recommendations*基于 Conclusion 提出建议（*部分报告不含此部分）
📝 语言特点：被动语态（passive voice）+ 第三人称（third person）→ 使报告客观、非个人化。常用时态：past simple / present perfect / present simple / present continuous。

Part Three · Analysis

📝 报告分析：语言特点判断

阅读以下报告片段，判断其语言特点。

Report on factors contributing to job satisfaction

In recent years, the problem of high staff turnover has become more acute in companies and organizations of various sectors. To engage and retain employees, boosting job satisfaction is increasingly viewed as a key strategy. In light of this, a survey on job satisfaction was conducted and 1031 people aged 18-45 participated in it. This report aims at presenting the findings of the survey, i.e. factors that contribute most to overall job satisfaction among the local workforce.

1.1 Job nature
Job nature refers to how interesting, challenging or exciting a job is. A remarkable difference was noted among different employee demographics, with Gen-Zers touting it as the most important job aspect.
这段报告使用了什么语言特点？（多选）
✅ Passive voice + third person — 例如 "a survey was conducted"（被动）、"1031 people participated"（第三人称）、"It is recommended"（被动+非人称）。报告必须 objective + impersonal。

Part Three · Common Mistakes

⚠️ 报告写作常见错误

点击卡片查看解析 →

错误 1：这句话哪里错了？

I think job satisfaction is very important. We should all try to be happy at work. I feel that companies need to do more.

💡 点击卡片查看解析

✅ 解析

问题：使用第一人称（I/we），语气主观、个人化。报告必须 objective + impersonal，用 passive voice + third person。

✓ Job satisfaction is considered a key factor in employee retention. It has been found that companies with higher job satisfaction levels tend to have lower turnover rates.

错误 2：这句话哪里错了？

Dear Mr. Ho, How are you today? I'm so excited about the competition! Can't wait! Fingers crossed!

💡 点击卡片查看解析

✅ 解析

问题：这是 memo，不是 letter。Memo 无 salutation、无 closing remark、语气要 formal/semi-formal、简洁不冗余。"How are you today?" / "I'm so excited" / "Can't wait" 都是冗余或过于随意。

✓ To: Mr. Ho / From: Chris Wong / Date: 15 May 20__ / Subject: Poetry writing competition / I would like to inform you that five members of the Poetry Society are going to enter this competition.
