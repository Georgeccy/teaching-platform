Task 2A · Understanding the Question

📋 认真审题，提取关键信息

Write about 400 words for the following topic: An article in the Hong Kong Daily reported on the violence found in many video games popular with young people. As a member of your school's IT Club, you believe today's game developers are socially responsible to educate young gamers. Write a letter to the editor of the Hong Kong Daily. Explain why you believe game developers have a responsibility to young people and how they might change their work to influence society in a positive way.
1. Role（你是谁）＋ 2. Audience（写给谁）
Role: a member of your school's IT Club
Audience: newspaper readers / the general public
3. Purpose（写作目的，可多选）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选 1 和 3。题目要求"explain why"（原因）+ "how they might change"（方案），并不要求讨论 benefits。
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ formal — 写给报社编辑、面向公众读者，语域正式
5. Tone（语气，可多选）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选：logical / objective / persuasive。编辑来信以理服人，不靠情感渲染或生动描写。

Part 3 · 思考 3.1–3.4

🧠 想论点：企业为什么要负责？

思考 3.1 一家企业能够赚钱，是不是因为社会给了它很多资源？具体有哪些资源？
是的。比如学校教育为企业培养并输送了人才；社会提供了稳定和安全的生产经营环境等。
思考 3.2 反过来，企业在享受社会提供的资源后，是否应该回馈相应的正面价值？
是的。企业享有权利，就应承担义务。
思考 3.3 如何确定一个主体是否应该对某件事负责？假如学校旁边的工地在上课时间施工，噪音影响同学听课，工地是否应该负责？
（工地的例子只是类比，便于理解）需要负责，因为工地的行为已给他人带来负面影响——"因为有影响，所以有责任"。开发商的游戏包含暴力元素，可能让年轻人在追寻刺激时上瘾乃至模仿，这一价值观层面的消极影响由开发商造成，所以需要负责。
思考 3.4 如果游戏开发商不对暴力内容负责，会有什么后果？
暴力内容加上游戏的奖励机制和强互动性，会让年轻人对此类内容更感兴趣、甚至上瘾、模仿，最终对其行为产生负面引导。

功能句式
第一句 · 说明写信目的• I am writing in response to the suggestion/proposal/opinion that + 话题内容
• I am writing to express my view on the suggestion/proposal/opinion that + 话题内容
第二句 · 表明立场/回应• While 让步承认目标话题的正面影响, I believe/suggest/consider that 抛出自己的观点立场.

思考 6.2 · PEER Formula

📐 PEER 论证公式

主体段1每句话的功能分别是什么？对照下表，回到上一页检查 fn-tag 标注：

要素引导问题中文功能
P (Point)What is your point?表明观点
E (Explain)Why / How?展开解释（为什么 / 如何）
E (Example)How / what?举例 / 列举 / 具体化
R (Result)So what? Why it matters?结果 / 影响 / 最终判断
📝 Notes：PEER 只是论证参考公式，不代表每部分都要单独写一句、或每部分只能出现一次。比如主体段1第二句，explain 和 example 放在一句中；最后一句再次出现并列原因阐述（further explain），进一步阐述"教育"和"社会责任"之间的联系。

思考 7.1 · Body Paragraph 2

🧠 主体段2：游戏影响行为与价值观

参考 PEER 公式，梳理论证思路——点击查看提示答案：

Point: 另一个原因是游戏会对年轻人的行为和价值观产生影响
Explain:（提示：这种影响和游戏本身的互动性是否有关？）
游戏具备强互动性，年轻人控制角色进行游戏的过程有很强的代入感，必然会受游戏内容影响。
Example:（提示：游戏中具体什么内容/机制常和暴力有关？）
游戏中的奖励/升级/排名机制，经常基于玩家控制角色进行暴力行为，如打斗/射击。
Result: 年轻人会觉得暴力行为是有趣的、可以接受的，形成错误价值观
Further result:（提示：回扣主题句，得出结论）
这一消极影响，决定了企业必须为此负责。

Language Bank · How to Explain a Reason

🔘 语言库：如何展开原因解释

类别句式
抛出原因论点的句式1. The first and foremost reason why … is 短语/that 从句 …
2. One major reason why … is 短语/that 从句 …
3. Another cause of sth. is 短语/that 从句 …
展开原因解释的句式1. Since/As/Because 原因从句, 结果主句.
2. Given that + 原因从句, 结果主句.
3. Due to/As a result of 原因短语, 结果主句.
展开结果的句式1. 原因主句, which can lead to 结果短语
2. 原因主句, leading to/resulting in 结果短语
3. This gives rise to ...
📝 使用提示：主体段2的 Point 用「Another reason why … is that …」，Explain 用「Since 从句, 主句」，Result 用「which can lead to …」——正好各对应一行。

思考 8.1–8.2 · Body Paragraph 3

🧠 方案从哪来？

思考 8.1 第二段提到游戏中的奖励/升级/排名常基于暴力行为获得，这部分可以做出什么调整？
开发者改变这种设计，奖励可以基于策略/合作等正面行为。
思考 8.2 除了改变游戏设计，能不能从接触途径方面提供方案？
可以增加年龄认证系统，或游戏内容分级展示。（→ 这就是主体段4的方案！）
🤔构思逻辑：从第二段找出的"问题机制"出发，反向设计解决方案——问题在奖励机制就改奖励机制，问题在接触途径就限制接触途径。

思考 8.3 · Sentence Ordering

🔀 将句子按论证逻辑排序

A. For example, developers could design games in a more responsible way.
B. There are several ways game developers can encourage more positive behaviour.
C. However, rather than rewarding players only for defeating enemies, games could place greater emphasis on teamwork, strategy and protecting teammates.
D. Many popular games, such as Call of Duty or Honor of Kings, are naturally competitive and involve fighting as part of the gameplay.
E. In games like Honor of Kings, success often depends on cooperation, and developers could strengthen this aspect to encourage communication and teamwork rather than focusing only on combat.
这 5 句话的正确排序是？
✅ B → A → D → C → E
论证逻辑：Main point(B) → Sub-point(A) → Explain+Example(D+C) → Further explain + Example(E)

附录 · Model Answer

📝 完整范文（词伙已高亮）

Dear editor,

I am writing as a member of my school's IT Club in response to your recent article about the violence found in many video games popular with young people. While video games can be exciting and enjoyable, I strongly believe that game developers have a social responsibility to educate young gamers and influence society in a more positive way.

First of all, game developers have a social responsibility to young people. Like all businesses, gaming companies rely heavily on social resources, including a stable operating environment, talented and creative workers, and supportive market access policies. Therefore, they should also make efforts to act responsibly and give back to society, such as promoting education among young people. Since young people are easily influenced during this stage of life, game developers should play a positive role in helping them grow in a healthy way.

Another reason why gaming companies should be responsible for violent content is that video games can influence young people's behavior and values. Since games are highly interactive, players become deeply involved in the characters and are easily affected by the game content. In many games, players receive rewards, upgrades, or higher rankings for violent actions such as fighting or shooting. As a result, young people may gradually see violence as exciting or acceptable, which can lead to distorted values. Therefore, gaming companies should take responsibility for the negative impacts their products may have on society.

There are several ways game developers can encourage more positive behaviour. For example, developers could design games in a more responsible way. Many popular games, such as Call of Duty or Honor of Kings, are naturally competitive and involve fighting as part of the gameplay. However, rather than rewarding players only for defeating enemies, games could place greater emphasis on teamwork, strategy and protecting teammates. In games like Honor of Kings, success often depends on cooperation, and developers could strengthen this aspect to encourage communication and teamwork rather than focusing only on combat.

Another way game developers can act more responsibly is to introduce stricter controls on who can access violent games. As many young players can still easily gain access to games that contain excessive violence, developers should place stronger restrictions on violent content. For example, age verification systems could be introduced, while accounts for younger players could have limited access to certain games or features. This would help reduce the possible negative influence of violent games on young people.

To sum up, I believe game developers do have a responsibility to young people because of their obligations to our society, as well as their strong influence on the younger generation. As an industry with such a powerful impact on society, game developers should make greater efforts to guide young gamers in a more positive direction. After all, entertainment should not only be enjoyable, but also socially responsible.

Yours faithfully,
Chris Wong
Member, IT Club
