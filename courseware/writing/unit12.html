Course Objectives

🎯 本节课目标

By the end of this lesson, students will be able to:

1. 了解并初步掌握 Review 的结构和语气特点

Understand the structure and tone of reviews — informative, evaluative and balanced

2. 掌握 Review 的段落组织方式以及段内常见展开方式

Master paragraph organization: Introduction → Features (Environment/Service/Food/Price) → Conclusion; each body paragraph follows Feature + Impact + Evaluation

3. 了解并尝试运用 "creativity and imagination" 要求的具体细节

Learn to use specific, authentic details (who / where / when / what happened) to enhance creativity

4. 掌握 feature（特点）与 impact（影响）常用句式和表达

Master sentence patterns for describing features and their impacts

Topic: You finally got the chance to eat at Hong Kong's hottest new restaurant, Dim Sum One. Write a review for Eat & Drink, an online food guide. Write about what you liked, what you didn't like and if you would recommend this restaurant. (~200 words)

Task 2A · Understanding the Question

📋 审题分析

1. Role（你是谁）
A customer / diner who has eaten at the restaurant
2. Audience（写给谁）
Readers of Eat & Drink; People who are interested in visiting the restaurant
3. Purpose（写作目的，多选）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选第一项和第三项。Review 的核心目的是评价餐厅并帮助读者做决定。
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ semi-formal — 线上美食指南，面向大众读者，语气适中
5. Tone（语气，可多选）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选 Informative + Evaluative。Review 需要提供信息并给出评价，而非中立报告或个人反思。

Part 3 · Brainstorming

🧠 想论点 ①

📍思考3.1: 根据题干信息，这篇 review 最适合选什么立场？请找出题目中的两个线索支持你的答案。
① 题目明确要求写 liked, didn't like — 学生需要同时讨论正面和负面的体验。
② hottest new restaurant 说明这是一家近期非常受欢迎、备受关注的餐厅。如果全篇都在批评，会与题目背景不太一致。
→ 最适合的立场：balanced / generally positive
📍思考3.2: 如果是你自己，更容易相信哪类 review？是使劲儿夸/使劲儿损的，还是相对客观平衡的？
相对平衡的 (balanced review)。因为真实的体验通常既有优点也有缺点。适当指出不足之处，会让评价显得更加可信和客观。因此，高分 review 往往不是一味称赞或批评，而是呈现较平衡的观点。

Part 3 · Brainstorming (cont.)

🧠 想论点 ②

📍思考3.3: 除了食物、服务和环境外，还可以加入哪些具体而生动的细节，使这篇 review 更有趣、更具个人特色？
具体地点 places (where) + 人物 people (who) + 时间 time/duration (when/how long) + 个人经历 personal experiences (what happened)。这些内容能够让文章显得更加真实、生动和有画面感。
📍思考3.4: 以下哪一句更适合出现在 Food Guide 上的 review？哪一句更适合出现在个人 Blog 上？
A. Blog 风格

I absolutely loved the service! The staff made me feel so welcome that I almost didn't want to leave, and I was already planning my next visit before I had even finished my meal.

B. Food Guide ✅

The service was friendly and attentive, and the food was reasonably priced. Overall, the restaurant offers good value for money.

Food Guide 上的 review 主要目的是：提供有用的信息 + 评价餐厅的优缺点 + 帮助读者决定是否前往，因此语气通常较理性、克制和客观。而个人 Blog 除了评价之外，还希望分享个人经历和感受，因此表达往往更加情绪化、生动和具有个人特色。

Part 5 · Introduction

✍️ 开头段范文精读

📍思考5.1: 开头段首句提到给出特定喜好的人群，有什么特别的好处吗？
涉及到评分标准里的 audience awareness 读者意识。在开头提及特定人群能够：①明确这家餐厅适合谁；②引发有相同兴趣读者的共鸣；③让推荐显得更自然、更可信。

P1 >>>>>>>> Introduction

If you enjoy ______, Dim Sum One is a restaurant worth considering. While my visit was ______, I ______ it has become one of Hong Kong's hottest new restaurants, and ______.
📝 写作要点：① If you enjoy..., ... is worth considering. 有条件推荐；② While + drawback, I could understand why..., and my overall impression was generally positive. 承认不足→肯定优点→给出总评。这是 Review 开头段的标准结构。

Structure & Notes · Body Paragraph Pattern

📌 Notes — Creativity & Imagination:
在 review 中，"creativity and imagination" 不一定来自天马行空的故事，而往往来自具体且真实的细节：

• people (who) — chefs, staff, family and friends
• time / duration (when / how long) — nearly 30 minutes, during my birthday dinner

📍思考7.1: 为什么这个版本比只写 "The staff were friendly and helpful." 更真实、更有画面感？
因为加入了 specific people (who) — chefs, staff, young customer，和 personal experiences (what happened) — came out to ask for feedback, sang birthday songs。读者不仅知道服务好，还能看到服务是如何体现出来的。

Homework

📝 课后作业: Outdoor Cinema Review

PART A For question 1, write about 200 words.

You recently watched a film at an outdoor, open-air cinema. Write a review for an online film guide that allows users to write their own reviews.
• Write about what you liked, what you did not like and if you would recommend watching films outdoors.

根据开头段结构模版，完成本题开头段，并根据提示把后面段落补充完成。

If you enjoy watching films in a more relaxed and unconventional setting, an open-air cinema can be a refreshing alternative to a traditional theatre. While the experience was not perfect, I can understand why many people enjoy outdoor screenings, and my overall impression was generally positive.

One thing I really liked was the surrounding environment. The cinema was set on a spacious grassy lawn, creating a peaceful and relaxing atmosphere. A cool evening breeze and the fresh scent of flowers made the experience even more enjoyable. Watching a film under the night sky felt special and offered something that indoor cinemas simply cannot provide.

Another highlight was the overall viewing experience. The screen was clear, the sound quality was good, and it was easy to find a seat with a decent view of the film. This made it easy to become immersed in the story from beginning to end.

However, there were a few areas for improvement. The chairs were not particularly comfortable, and I started to feel tired after sitting for a long period of time. In addition, tickets were more expensive than those at regular cinemas, and the snack selection was fairly limited. Considering the extra cost, I expected slightly better facilities.

Overall, I would recommend trying an open-air cinema at least once, especially if you enjoy outdoor activities and a unique viewing atmosphere. Although it may not offer the same level of comfort or value for money as a traditional cinema, it provides a memorable experience that is well worth trying.

Appendix · Model Answer

📝 范文：Dim Sum One Restaurant Review

Task: Write a review for Eat & Drink, an online food guide. (~200 words / 350 words full version)

If you enjoy traditional Chinese dim sum in a warm and comfortable environment, Dim Sum One is a restaurant worth considering. While my visit was not perfect, I could understand why it has become one of Hong Kong's hottest new restaurants, and my overall impression was generally positive.

One thing I really liked was the dining environment. The restaurant is located near Victoria Harbour, offering a lovely sea view that customers can enjoy while eating. Inside, the atmosphere feels warm and comfortable, making it a relaxing place to share a meal with family and friends. However, it is tucked away from the main street, and I spent nearly 30 minutes trying to find it, which was rather frustrating.

The service was another highlight. The staff were friendly and attentive, and they genuinely seemed to care about their customers. Chefs occasionally came out to ask for feedback, which made the experience feel more personal. I was also touched to see the staff singing birthday songs to a young customer. With special arrangements available for children's birthday parties, it is easy to see why the restaurant is particularly suitable for families.

As for the food, Dim Sum One focuses on traditional Chinese dim sum. One dish that stood out was the "Fantasy Bun", which allows customers to choose their own ingredients and sauces. Freshly made and full of flavour, it tasted even better than similar dishes I had tried elsewhere. That said, I was a little disappointed to discover that some items, such as fishballs and siu mai, were bought from supermarkets rather than made in-house.

There was also good news when it came to price. With the current 30% discount offered during its first year, a meal costs around $60 per person, making it an affordable option for many families and offering good value for money.

Overall, I would recommend giving Dim Sum One a try, especially if you are looking for friendly service, a cosy atmosphere and reasonably priced dim sum. Although there is still room for improvement in some of the dishes, it offers an enjoyable dining experience that is well worth trying.
