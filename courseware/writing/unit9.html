Part 2 · Understanding the Question

📋 审题五要素

You recently read an article about a supermarket asking customers to donate food to poor families. Having collected some information about the problem of food waste in Hong Kong, you would like to persuade the supermarkets, food shops and restaurants in your local area into donating food to the poor. Write a letter to the supermarkets, food shops and restaurants about the initiative.
1. Role（你是谁）+ 2. Audience（写给谁）
Role：A concerned member of the community / a local resident
Audience：Supermarkets, food shops and restaurants in the local area
3. Purpose（写作目的）
Persuade supermarkets, food shops and restaurants to donate surplus food to poor families
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ formal — 写给本地商家的正式信件，需礼貌而正式
5. Tone（语气，可多选）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选：logical / persuasive / polite。说服信以理服人、以礼待人，不需要 narrative/descriptive 的画面感。

Part 3 · Brainstorming

🧠 思考 3.1 & 3.2

🤔思考3.1：评估一个方法或措施时，可以从哪几个方面思考？（复习 Unit 1）
评估措施的四个角度：
① 这个方法真的做得到吗？Feasibility 可行性
② 这个方法真的有效吗？Effectiveness 效果
③ 会不会带来新的问题？Drawbacks 副作用
④ 是否有必要这样做？Necessity 必要性
🤔思考3.2：为什么 supermarkets, food shops and restaurants 有必要捐食物？
① 每天仍有大量可食用食物被浪费
② 许多低收入家庭难以负担日常餐食
③ 作为食品相关企业，他们有环境责任（减少食物浪费）和社会责任（帮助有需要的人）

Part 3 · Brainstorming

🧠 思考 3.3 & 3.4

🤔思考3.3：supermarkets, food shops and restaurants 捐赠食物是否容易实施？
容易。原因：
① 已有资源 have access to food supplies
② 有专业知识 know how to store and handle food safely
③ 成本较低 can work with nearby charities or allow families to collect the food directly
→ 企业能以较低成本快速而有效地将食物送到有需要的人手中。
🤔思考3.4：这个计划能为 supermarkets, food shops and restaurants 带来什么好处？
三类好处：
① 对环境：减少食物浪费和对环境造成的影响
② 对社会：帮助贫困家庭获得食物
③ 对企业：提升企业形象和声誉，展现社会责任感，从而赢得顾客的信任和支持

📌 评估措施时，除了考虑它是否有效解决问题（effectiveness），也可以分析它带来的额外好处（added benefits）

Part 5 · Opening Paragraph

✍️ 开头段：三步走

🌟 Task 5A：点击 ① 的空白，补全段首句（写信目的）。

② PROBLEMFood waste has become one of the most serious environmental problems in Hong Kong, while many low-income families still struggle to afford daily meals.

功能句式
第一句说明写信目的I am writing to urge + sb + to do sth.
我写信是为了呼吁某人采取行动。
第二句介绍问题背景Problem A has become one of the most serious ... problems, while Problem B continues to affect ...
介绍当前存在的问题和现状。
第三句说明对方可发挥的作用As ..., you can play an important role in ...
说明对方能够发挥的重要作用。

附录 · Model Answer

📝 范文：Persuasive Letter — Food Donation Initiative

Task: Write a letter to the supermarkets, food shops and restaurants about the food donation initiative.（约 472 words）

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to urge supermarkets, food shops and restaurants in our local area to support a food donation initiative. Food waste has become one of the most serious environmental problems in Hong Kong, while many low-income families still struggle to afford daily meals. As food-related businesses, you can play an important role in reducing food waste and helping people in need.

First of all, as food-related businesses, you have both an environmental responsibility to reduce food waste and a social responsibility to support people in need. Every day, large amounts of edible food are thrown away instead of being put to good use. If this situation continues, valuable resources such as water, energy and labour used to produce food will be wasted, while our landfills will become full much earlier than expected. At the same time, many low-income families still cannot afford to buy enough food for themselves, and some children even go to school without having a proper meal. By donating surplus food, businesses can help provide support to those who need it most.

Food donation is a realistic and easy initiative for food-related businesses to support. Many food businesses already have access to food supplies and often have surplus items that are still suitable for consumption. This includes products close to their best-before dates, unsold bread, leftover cooked food and unused ingredients. In addition, food-related businesses understand how to store, transport and handle food safely, making the donation process much easier. The cost is usually low because businesses can work with nearby charities or community groups, while some families may even collect the food directly. Therefore, food can be distributed to people in need quickly and efficiently.

Supporting this initiative can bring significant benefits to both society and your business. For the community, food donation helps ensure that edible food is put to good use rather than wasted, while families facing financial difficulties can receive much-needed support. Even a small donation from a business can make a huge difference to a family in need. For your company, regular food donation can also improve your public image. Today, many customers prefer businesses that act responsibly and care about society. By taking part in this initiative, your company can demonstrate that it is a caring and socially responsible organization. Customers are more likely to trust and support businesses that are willing to help others. As a result, food donation can strengthen your reputation while creating a positive impact on the wider community.

We firmly believe that together we can make a real and lasting difference. Therefore, I strongly encourage your business to join this food donation initiative and work with nearby charities or community groups. Your support can help reduce food waste, support families in need and create a more caring future for everyone.

Yours faithfully,

Chris Wong
