Part 1 · Understanding the Question

📋 审题：分析写作要求

While tai chi is a popular activity in Hong Kong, it is less known in some parts of the world. Write an article for International Travel magazine introducing the benefits of tai chi to tourists.

🌟 Task 2A: 先自己想，再点击揭晓 ↓

1. Role（你是谁）
A writer for International Travel magazine（旅游杂志撰稿人）
2. Audience（写给谁）
International travellers / tourists who may not be familiar with tai chi（对太极不熟悉的国际游客）
3. Purpose（写作目的）
To introduce the benefits of tai chi and encourage tourists to try it（介绍好处 + 鼓励尝试）
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ semi-formal — 杂志文章面向大众读者，语气自然友好、不过于随意，也不过于学术。
5. Tone（语气，可多选）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选：Informative / Engaging / Positive / Vivid & descriptive。
✗ Objective 不合适——travel article 要「夸夸模式」吸引读者来体验，不是中立报道。
△ 注意：与 blog 相比，travel article 的生动描写较为克制，主要作用是增强 engaging 的效果，而非表达强烈个人情感。

Part 2 · Brainstorming

🧠 想论点：铺垫 & 太极的好处

🤔先思考，再揭晓——这些论点将组成文章的主体段！
思考 3.1 对游客来说，来香港的常规操作是做什么？
Shopping, food, famous landmarks / tourist spots（购物、美食、著名地标）

△ 写作技巧：利用读者熟悉的旅游活动作铺垫，再引出意想不到的体验（unexpected alternative），是 travel article 常见开头。
思考 3.2 打太极对人自身有哪些好处？
● reduce stress, restore energy, improve well-being（降压、充电、促进健康）
● relax the body and mind, enjoy the peaceful / present moment（放松身心、放慢节奏、享受平静）

Part 2 · Brainstorming

🧠 想论点：文化 · 社交 · 吸引力

思考 3.3 旅行除了娱乐，还有其他目的吗？
① 拓宽眼界 / 获取知识；② 了解当地文化与价值观。
这就是利弊通用论点第4点 information & knowledge，准确说是 cultural understanding。
思考 3.4 旅行过程中与当地人互动有什么好处？
connect with local people, experience everyday life / lifestyle, make travel more enjoyable and meaningful

⭐ 最重要：通过互动，游客才能真正深入了解当地人的日常生活和生活方式——而不是像跟旅行团那样只是景点打卡（最表层的旅行，很难真正了解文化）。
思考 3.5 一篇旅行杂志的文章，作者可以用什么方法吸引读者？
① 用积极正面的词汇（benefits「夸夸模式」）→ 让读者觉得这种体验很美好，产生尝试的兴趣；
② 用较为生动的表达（如感官描写、祈使句）→ 让读者身临其境，更容易投入。

Part 5 · Introduction — 3 Openings

📐 介绍型 Article 开头的三类写法

方法作用例句
1. 与常规期待形成对比
Expectation → However		 先提及读者熟悉的常规体验，再用 However 引出意想不到、更特别的体验。 When traveling, many people focus on famous landmarks, shopping and food. However, to truly understand a place, it is equally important to experience its culture and lifestyle.
当……时，很多人往往会…。然而，……同样重要。
2. 提问式开头
Question Opening		 用 Have you ever...? 邀请读者思考或想象，拉近与读者的距离。 Have you ever wondered how to experience a quieter side of Hong Kong? 你是否曾想过如何……？
Have you ever wondered what it feels like to…? 你是否曾想过…是一种怎样的体验？
3. 强烈表达／祈使句开头
Imperative		 用祈使句或充满感染力的表达，立即吸引读者的注意。 Prepare your taste buds for a feast as you explore Hong Kong's vibrant food culture. 准备好…吧，当…时。
Get ready for the excitement as the dragon boats race across the water. 准备好迎接…吧，当…时。

🔍 思考 6.2：哪句更有画面感？

🤔对比 A、B 两句，哪一句表达更生动、让读者更有画面感？为什么？
✅ B 更生动！主句前加入过去分词短语（Often seen in …），让读者仿佛看见人们在公园和海滨练习太极的场景。

📝 范文：Discover Tai Chi in Hong Kong

Task: Write an article for International Travel magazine introducing the benefits of tai chi to tourists.

Slow Down and Breathe: Discover Tai Chi in Hong Kong

When travelling, many people focus on famous landmarks, shopping and food. However, to truly understand a place, it is equally important to experience its culture and lifestyle. In Hong Kong, one unique activity that offers both relaxation and cultural insight is tai chi. Often seen in public parks and along the waterfront, this traditional practice provides visitors with a refreshing alternative to the fast-paced rhythm of typical travel.

Unlike high-intensity sports, tai chi is a gentle form of exercise that involves slow and controlled movements. It is especially suitable for travellers who may not be used to intense physical activity. For those who feel tired after long days of sightseeing, a short tai chi session can help relax the body, release tension and restore energy. Imagine practising tai chi by Victoria Harbour as the sun rises, feeling the cool breeze on your face and moving gently with the rhythm of your breath. Such moments allow travellers to slow down, appreciate a quieter side of Hong Kong and fully enjoy the present moment.

What makes tai chi even more special is its cultural significance. It is deeply rooted in Chinese philosophy, particularly the idea of balance and harmony between the body and the mind. Each movement reflects a sense of control, patience and awareness. By learning a few simple movements, visitors are not only exercising but also gaining a deeper understanding of traditional Chinese values. In this way, tai chi offers a more meaningful cultural experience than simply visiting historical sites or taking photos.

Tai chi also offers visitors a valuable chance to connect with local people. It is especially suitable for travellers who want to experience everyday life beyond the usual tourist attractions. Locals are often welcoming and willing to guide beginners through basic movements, allowing visitors to interact with the community in a natural way. By joining small groups in public spaces and following along, visitors can not only learn simple movements but also build connections with local people. Through these small, authentic encounters, travellers can truly experience everyday life in Hong Kong.

In conclusion, tai chi is far more than just a form of exercise—it is a relaxing, cultural and deeply memorable experience. For travellers seeking something beyond typical sightseeing, trying tai chi offers a unique way to engage both the body and the mind. It may well become one of the most rewarding and meaningful moments of their journey.
