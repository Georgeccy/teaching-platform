Task 2A · Understanding the Question

📋 审题分析

1. Role（你是谁）
A concerned citizen / reader of the Hong Kong Post
2. Audience（写给谁）
The editor and readers of the Hong Kong Post
3. Purpose（写作目的）
To explain the reasons for the decline in tourist numbers
To suggest ways to attract visitors back to Hong Kong
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ formal — LTE 是写给编辑和读者的正式信件
5. Tone（语气，可多选）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选：logical / objective / persuasive。分析原因和提出建议需要逻辑清晰、客观理性、有说服力。

Part 3 · Brainstorming

🧠 想论点 ①

📍思考3.1: 占比最多的香港访客是哪里的？
大多数来港访客来自内地。历年数据显示，约四分之三的香港访客来自内地。这能够帮助我们意识到：香港旅游业的变化很大程度上与内地游客人数的变化有关。
📍思考3.2: 过去，许多游客来香港的主要目的是什么？
过去，香港被视为一个现代化、国际化和高度发达的城市。许多游客来香港购物、购买国际品牌商品，以及体验其他地方较难接触到的国际化生活方式。

Part 3 · Brainstorming

🧠 想论点 ②

📍思考3.3: 为什么现在游客有更多旅游选择？
交通的发展以及网上旅游资讯的普及，使国际旅游比以前方便得多。如今，游客可以轻松了解不同国家和地区的旅游信息，并前往更多目的地，例如日本、韩国和新加坡。
📍思考3.4: 现在来香港购物的人为什么变少了？
① 国际品牌现在更容易买到：现在人们可以通过网购平台或在内地城市购买国际品牌商品，因此不一定需要专程到香港购物。
② 人们的消费习惯改变了：人们现在更愿意把钱花在体验上，而不是奢侈品实物上。
📝 三大变化角度：
思考角度具体方面
1. Social Change（社会变化）Globalization → More travel choices
Improvements in transportation
2. Technological Change（科技变化）Easier access to travel information online
Online shopping / E-commerce platforms
Changing consumer habits
3. Human Change（人的变化）Preference for experiences over products
Reduced interest in luxury goods

Part 4 · Overall Structure

📊 文章结构

原因与建议类 LTE 采用六段式结构：

SectionMain Content
IntroductionState the issue and outline the response (reasons + suggestions)
Reason 1Why do tourists have more choices now?
Reason 2Why is shopping less attractive than before?
Suggestion 1How can Hong Kong promote its existing strengths?
Suggestion 2How can Hong Kong create new attractions?
ConclusionSummarize the main points
📝 结构要点：前两段分析原因（Reason 1: 竞争增加; Reason 2: 购物吸引力下降），中间两段提出建议（Suggestion 1: 推广现有优势; Suggestion 2: 创造新吸引力），最后总结。

Appendix · Model Answer

📝 范文：Letter to the Editor

Task: Explain reasons for tourism decline & suggest solutions (~485 words, 6 paragraphs)

Dear Editor,

I am writing in response to an article in the Hong Kong Post stating that the number of tourists visiting Hong Kong has fallen. In my opinion, there are several reasons behind this trend, and a number of measures can be taken to attract visitors back to the city.

One reason for the decline is that Hong Kong now faces stronger competition from other destinations. In the past, Hong Kong was one of the most popular places for travellers from Mainland China and other parts of Asia because it was seen as a modern, international and highly developed city. However, international travel has become much easier than before due to improvements in transportation and easier access to travel information online. Nowadays, tourists can choose from many attractive destinations, such as Japan, South Korea and Singapore. These places offer different cultures, cuisines and travel experiences, making them attractive alternatives to Hong Kong. As a result, Hong Kong is no longer the only attractive option for travellers.

Another reason is that Hong Kong's shopping appeal has become weaker. In the past, many tourists visited Hong Kong mainly for shopping because certain products were cheaper or easier to find. However, people's shopping habits and spending preferences have changed significantly. Nowadays, international brands are widely available in mainland cities, while online shopping allows consumers to purchase products from around the world without travelling. In addition, many people no longer view luxury goods as the main purpose of travelling. Instead, they prefer spending money on meaningful experiences, such as local food, cultural activities and outdoor adventures. As a result, shopping is no longer as strong an attraction as it once was.

To attract tourists back, Hong Kong should promote its unique culture, lifestyle and natural scenery. Hong Kong has many attractions that cannot easily be found elsewhere, including traditional festivals, local food, cha chaan tengs, the Star Ferry and scenic hiking routes such as the MacLehose Trail. The government could use travel videos and social media campaigns to showcase these attractions to both international and mainland visitors. This would help more people discover Hong Kong's unique character and encourage them to visit the city.

Another effective way is to develop more experience-based attractions and events. For example, Hong Kong could organise more concerts, music festivals, cultural exhibitions and pop-culture attractions such as character-themed exhibitions and large-scale harbourfront displays. By holding these events regularly throughout the year, Hong Kong can attract different groups of visitors, especially young people and families. This would give tourists more reasons to visit the city.

In conclusion, the decline in visitor numbers is mainly caused by stronger competition and changing consumer habits. However, Hong Kong still has much to offer. By making better use of its unique attractions and creating new reasons for people to visit, the city can attract more visitors and remain a popular destination in the future.

Yours faithfully,
Chris Wong
