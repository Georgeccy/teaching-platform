You are a candidate in a speech competition. Write a speech explaining the causes of employment discrimination against disabled people in Hong Kong, how this discrimination affects their financial independence, and provide solutions to this problem.

Task 2A · Understanding the Question

📋 审题分析

1. Role（你是谁）
A candidate in a speech competition
2. Audience（写给谁）
Judges, teachers, fellow students
3. Purpose（写作目的，可多选）
✅ 四项全选 — 题目要求涵盖现象描述、原因分析、经济影响和解决方案。
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ semi-formal — Speech 面向评委和同学，正式但不刻板
5. Tone（语气，可多选）
✅ 应选：logical / engaging / persuasive。Speech 需要有逻辑地说服听众，同时保持互动感。

Part 3 · Brainstorming

📍思考3.1: 残疾人就业为什么会被歧视？是否有社会观念方面的原因？

R (Result)So what? Why it matters?结果 / 影响 / 最终判断
📍思考6.1: 对比以下句子，勾选更适合 speech 的一句
A. 更适合 Speech ✅

• find suitable jobs 比 access equal employment opportunities 更口语化
• Some employers automatically assume... 用词更简单
• For example, a hearing-impaired applicant... 句子较短，容易跟随

B. 更适合 Essay

• prevent disabled people from accessing equal employment opportunities
• Many employers perceive disabled individuals as being less capable...
• For instance, ... despite possessing qualifications comparable to...

Appendix · Model Answer

📝 范文：Employment Discrimination Speech

Good morning, judges, teachers and fellow students.

In today's society, more and more people are concerned about social equality. Unfortunately, many disabled people still face discrimination when seeking employment in Hong Kong. Today I'll explore the causes of such discrimination, its impact on financial independence, and possible solutions to the problem.

One important reason is that many people still have misconceptions about disabled individuals. Some employers automatically assume that disabled people cannot work as efficiently as others. As a result, they may overlook capable applicants simply because of their disabilities. For example, a hearing-impaired applicant may be rejected for a computer programming position, even though the job mainly involves working on a computer and requires little verbal communication. Therefore, these stereotypes create unfair barriers and make it harder for disabled people to find suitable jobs.

Another reason for employment discrimination is that some employers are worried about the cost of hiring disabled workers. They may think that they need to spend extra money on workplace adjustments, such as installing ramps or providing special equipment. Some employers also worry that disabled employees may require additional support or training. For example, a company may hesitate to hire a visually impaired worker because it believes that extra training sessions and communication support will increase its operating costs. As a result, these financial concerns may discourage employers from giving disabled people a fair chance, leading to discrimination in the workplace.

More importantly, employment discrimination has a serious impact on the financial independence of disabled people. When disabled people are denied job opportunities, it becomes much harder for them to find stable employment and earn a steady income. Without enough income, many of them have no choice but to rely on their families or government support to meet their daily needs, including rent, transportation and other living expenses. Consequently, employment discrimination makes it much more challenging for disabled people to support themselves and live independently.

So, what can we do to address this problem? Both the government and employers have an important role to play. On the one hand, the government should strengthen anti-discrimination laws and provide incentives for companies that hire disabled workers. On the other hand, employers should focus on applicants' abilities and qualifications rather than their disabilities. They should also create a more inclusive working environment by improving accessibility and providing vocational training programmes for disabled people. If both the government and businesses take action, we can reduce discrimination and help more disabled people enter the workforce.

To conclude, employment discrimination against disabled people is mainly caused by negative stereotypes and employers' concerns about additional costs. Unfortunately, this unfair treatment limits job opportunities and makes it harder for disabled people to achieve financial independence. If the government and employers work together, we can build a fairer and more inclusive Hong Kong where everyone has an equal chance to succeed.
