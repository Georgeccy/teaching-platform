Task Analysis

📋 审题：Suggestion Form 考什么？

You live in a housing estate that has not undergone any renovation work for a long time. The estate management is currently planning a renovation and has distributed a suggestion form to residents asking for their views on how to improve the estate. Fill in the suggestion form below.
1. Role（你的身份）——你是谁？
A resident of the housing estate（该屋苑的一位住户）
2. Audience（读者）——写给谁看？
Estate Management（屋苑管理处）— 因此语气要礼貌、正式、有建设性
3. Purpose（写作目的）——为什么写？
To give suggestions on how to improve the estate（就如何改善屋苑提出建议）— 每个建议必须附带理由
4. Register（语域）——用什么语气？
✅ semi-formal — 给管理处的建议表，语气正式但不刻板，体现建设性

Structure

📐 Suggestion Form 的黄金结构

每个建议 = Suggestion（建议） + Reason(s)（理由）。理由解释建议的好处。

组成部分功能与示例
Suggestion 建议明确写出你建议做什么
I suggest more security staff guard the estate.
Reason 理由解释为什么这样做有好处
This would stop people who don't live here from entering.
Additional Reason 补充理由进一步增强说服力
In addition, having more security staff on duty would make us feel safer.
📝 写作要点：Part A 建议表通常写 3 个建议，每个建议 2-3 句话（建议 + 理由）。总字数约 160 词。
Security patrol: I suggest more security staff guard the estate. Reasons: This would stop people who don't live here from entering. In addition, having more security staff on duty would make us feel safer.

Part Three · Argumentative Writing

📐 什么是 Argumentative Writing？

🤔先想一想：下面哪句话更符合议论文的特点？点击你的选择！
✅ B 是正确的！议论文需要用 facts（事实） 和 evidence（证据） 支撑观点，而不是空洞的 "I think"。
📖 定义：Argumentative writing presents an opinion about an issue and tries to persuade the reader to agree. It must include counter-arguments（反驳观点） to show you have considered different points of view.

Part Three · 5 Steps

📐 议论文写作五步法

步骤具体操作
Step 1: Read the question确定你的立场：同意还是反对？你的观点是什么？
Step 2: Brainstorm写下正反两面的观点，包括 counter-arguments（反驳观点）
Step 3: Organize your ideas按 Introduction → Body (3-4 paragraphs) → Conclusion 组织
Step 4: Draft考虑 content, structure, tone（formal / informal / semi-formal）
Step 5: Check and edit检查语法、拼写、逻辑连贯性
⚠️ 重要提醒：Argumentative writing should not be angry, nor should it mock other opinions. Use counterarguments sensibly and respectfully.

Part Three · Structure

📊 议论文三段式结构

段落功能与要求
Introduction简要陈述你的观点（Briefly state your opinion）
Body写 3-4 段，每段一个新观点 + 支撑信息。不必每段都有 counter-argument，但适当位置必须包含。
Conclusion总结你的立场和主要观点。不要引入新观点。
Title: Having the death penalty again will not stop crime rates from rising

Introduction: With the recent rise in violent crime, some people are supporting the return of the death penalty... We do not agree with this view and would like to explain why.

Body: There is no evidence that the death penalty reduces violent crime... Finally, what happens when someone is wrongly put to death?

Conclusion: To conclude, the death penalty is not an effective way with which to tackle violent crime. It should not be re-introduced.

Part Three · Cloze Practice

📝 辩论稿填空：选择最佳选项

阅读辩论稿片段，选出最合适的词语填入空白处。

With the recent rise in violent crime, some people are supporting the ______ of the death penalty.
✅ return — "return" 表示恢复、重新引入，符合语境。"order" 意为命令，不搭配。
They use the old argument that capital punishment will reduce ______.
✅ violent crime — reduce 的宾语应该是犯罪率/犯罪现象，而不是罪犯本身。
______ no evidence that the death penalty reduces violent crime.
✅ There is — There is no evidence that... 是固定句型，表示"没有证据表明……"
In Sweden, ______, where the death penalty was abolished more than 30 years ago, violent crime has not increased.
✅ on the other hand — 表示对比，引出与前面相反的例子。
______, what happens when someone is wrongly put to death?
✅ Finally, — 用于引出最后一个论点，表示递进关系。

Part Three · Common Mistakes

⚠️ 议论文常见错误

点击卡片查看解析 →

错误 1：这句话哪里错了？

I think the death penalty is very bad and it should not be allowed because it is wrong.

💡 点击卡片查看解析

✅ 解析

问题：只有观点（opinion），没有证据（evidence）。议论文需要 facts + opinions 结合。

✓ There is no evidence that the death penalty reduces violent crime. Statistics show that crime rates rise as a population grows.

错误 2：这句话哪里错了？

Some people say the death penalty is good, but I don't care what they think. They are stupid.

💡 点击卡片查看解析

✅ 解析

问题：语气不礼貌，攻击对方。议论文应该尊重不同观点，用 counter-argument 理性反驳。

✓ While some argue that the death penalty deters crime, there is no conclusive evidence to support this claim.
