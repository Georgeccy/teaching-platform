Part 1 · Understanding the Question

📋 Task 2A：分析题目的写作要求

题目背景：毕业纪念册委员会向 6C 班征集留言。你是九龙中学（Kowloon Secondary School）6C 班的学生，需完成 yearbook entry。
1. Role（你是谁）
A graduating student in Class 6C at Kowloon Secondary School.
九龙中学 6C 班的毕业班学生。
2. Audience（写给谁）
Fellow graduates and future readers (including yourself).
同届毕业生与未来的读者（也包括多年后的自己）。
3. Purpose（写作目的，可多选）
✅ 第 1、3 项 ✓ 是题目要求的写作目的：reflect on and preserve meaningful memories 与 express personal feelings and appreciation 都符合 yearbook entry 的回顾与情感表达目的。第 2 项 inform readers ... in an objective way 是客观报道，不符合 yearbook 的回顾与情感表达目的。
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ semi-formal — 毕业纪念册面向同学与未来的自己，语气正式但不刻板。
5. Tone（语气，可多选）
✅ reflective ✓ · nostalgic ✓ · sincere / appreciative ✓。Objective 不符合——yearbook entry 重在个人情感。

The subject I enjoyed studying was undoubtedly my elective, Visual Arts. It was not quite a prevalent choice in our class but I found studying Visual Arts was really interesting and helpful to my process of creating. During lessons, not only would we learn about the drawing skills and technique but also the knowledge of art history which is helpful when doing art appreciation. By learning art history, I got precious chance to understand different famous artists, for example Van Gogh, Monet, Andy Warhol — you name it. Getting opportunity to study their artwork broaden my horizon to learn a myriad of drawing style and painting technique, which gave me tons of inspiration to start my own piece of artwork. Task 5.1 · Enjoy vs Challenging

🔍 5.1：challenging 写法有什么不同？

阅读下面这段 found English challenging 的范例：

Looking back on my secondary school years, English was undoubtedly the subject I found the most challenging. At first, speaking in English made me nervous, and I often worried about making mistakes. However, step by step, I became more confident, and every improvement gave me a sense of achievement. Although English was demanding at times, no two lessons ever felt the same. Stories, discussions and role plays constantly pushed me out of my comfort zone and encouraged me to try new things. Most importantly, English taught me that growth often comes through challenges. It reminded me not to give up when progress seems slow and to believe in my own potential. These lessons have stayed with me long after I left the classroom.
🤔想一想：它和 enjoy studying 的写法有什么不同吗？
结构相同，内容也基本相同，都从「挑战与成就感」「不同体验」和「成长」三方面展开；不同之处只是 enjoy 更强调「喜欢的原因」，而 challenging 更强调「挑战带来的成长」。
（句式有区别的部分已加粗标注）

Part 6 · Brainstorming

🧠 6.1 / 6.2：怎样写好 Student Life？

困难时刻（学业压力、挑战、挫折）；快乐时刻（日常趣事、庆祝、活动）。

Appendix · Model Answer

📝 范文附录 Model Answer

Task：Write a yearbook entry for Class 6C (1.1 + 1.2).

1.1 A 版 — enjoyed studying (English)

Looking back on my secondary school years, English was undoubtedly the subject I enjoyed the most. At first, speaking in English made me nervous, but step by step, I became more confident, and every improvement gave me a sense of achievement. What I loved most about English was that no two lessons ever felt the same. Stories, discussions and role plays always brought laughter and surprises. Most importantly, English helped me grow as a person. It taught me that progress does not happen overnight and that making mistakes is simply part of learning. These lessons have stayed with me long after I left the classroom.

1.1 B 版 — found challenging (English)

Looking back on my secondary school years, English was undoubtedly the subject I found the most challenging. At first, speaking in English made me nervous, and I often worried about making mistakes. However, step by step, I became more confident, and every improvement gave me a sense of achievement. Although English was demanding at times, no two lessons ever felt the same. Stories, discussions and role plays constantly pushed me out of my comfort zone and encouraged me to try new things. Most importantly, English taught me that growth often comes through challenges. It reminded me not to give up when progress seems slow and to believe in my own potential. These lessons have stayed with me long after I left the classroom.

1.2 — Student Life (English)

What I will miss most about Class 6C is the sense of togetherness we shared. 6C was more than just a class; it was a group of people who stood by one another through both stressful and joyful times. That bond is something I will always treasure.

School life was not always easy. Projects, deadlines and exams often left us feeling stressed and overwhelmed. However, no one in 6C had to face these challenges alone. Some classmates patiently explained difficult homework problems, while others shared their notes or offered words of encouragement before exams. With such support, even the toughest days became easier.

I will also miss the joyful moments we created together. We laughed at inside jokes, shared snacks under the stairs and sang together when PE lessons were cancelled by rain. These small moments turned ordinary school days into unforgettable memories.

Looking back, the greatest gift of 6C was not the lessons or the grades, but the people who walked beside me throughout the journey. Those memories of standing side by side through both good times and difficult moments are something I will carry with me long after leaving school.
