Part One · Leaflet Genre

📖 什么是 Leaflet（传单）？

A leaflet is a small printed sheet or folded piece of paper that provides information or advertises something. It is usually distributed for free in public places.

① Distributed for free

免费派发 — 和报纸不同，传单不收费，放在公共场所供人取阅

② Used to advertise

广告宣传 — 目的通常是宣传某个产品、服务、活动或信息

③ Give information

提供信息 — 告知读者需要知道的事实、提示或建议

Which of the following are correct about leaflets?
① They are handed out for free.
② They are sold in bookshops.
③ They are used to advertise or give information.
✅ ① and ③ only — 传单免费派发（not sold），用于广告或提供信息。② 错误 — 传单不在书店出售。

Part One · Leaflet Format

📐 Leaflet 的格式要素

格式要素功能与说明
Headings 标题大标题吸引注意力，告诉读者主题是什么
e.g. "Stay Safe Online!"
Subheadings 小标题将内容分段，引导读者浏览
e.g. "Benefits of the Internet"
Bullet points 项目符号用要点形式列出关键信息，方便快速阅读
Eye-catching design通过加粗、颜色、图片、留白等视觉手段抓取注意力

Part One · Tone & Register

🎭 Leaflet 的语气选择

传单的语气取决于目的和受众——给同学的传单可以用 friendly tone，给政府的提案必须 formal。

Formal 正式

严肃的社会议题、政府宣传
e.g. "Citizens are advised to exercise caution when sharing personal information."

Semi-formal 半正式

校园活动、公共信息服务
e.g. "We recommend that you follow these simple tips for a safer online experience."

Informal 非正式

儿童读物、同龄人传单
e.g. "Hey! Want to learn some cool internet tricks? Read on!"

A leaflet on Internet safety for teenagers should use what tone?
✅ Semi-formal — 给青少年看的网络安全传单，语气半正式：友善但不随意，专业但不刻板。

Part Two · Proposal Genre

📄 什么是 Proposal（提案）？

A proposal is a formal document that presents ideas or suggestions and tries to persuade the reader to accept them. It is well-organised with clear sections and sub-headings.

① Present ideas / suggestions

提出想法或建议 — 不是评论，不是投诉，而是建设性地提出"应该怎么做"

② Persuade readers

说服读者 — 你需要让读者相信你的建议是可行的、有好处的

③ Well-organised

结构清晰 — 有固定的抬头格式（To/From/Subject）和分段小标题

💡 Proposal 的核心 = 提案 + 说服：你需要让读者相信你的建议是可行的、有好处的。和 Letter to the Editor 不同，Proposal 有固定格式。

Part Two · Proposal Format

📋 Proposal 的格式结构

组成部分功能与示例
To / From / Subject抬头信息：______
To: Ms Chan, Head of IT   From: Chris Wong   Subject: Proposal for a photo-sharing platform
Introduction说明提案______
The purpose of this proposal is to...
Body (sub-headings)分段介绍______，每段一个小标题
Conclusion总结______
To conclude, this proposal would benefit...
Closing remark礼貌结束，表达______
I hope these suggestions are useful and will be taken into consideration.
⚠️ 一定要注意格式！考试中格式不对直接扣 Organisation 分。Useful phrases:
• The purpose of this proposal is to...
• To conclude, this proposal would be of benefit to...
• I hope these suggestions are useful.

Part Two · Language Features

✍️ Proposal 的语言特点

提案的语言比传单更正式、更有说服力。以下每道题的题干隐藏了一个关键语言特点——先猜，再点 ✅ 看答案揭晓：

1. A proposal is usually _______: the benefits of each suggestion or idea need to be clearly stated so that the reader is persuaded to support it.
👉 这个语言特点叫什么？
Persuasive language（说服性语言） — 明确陈述每个建议的好处，让读者被说服去支持提案。
e.g. This would allow students to share their work more easily, encouraging greater collaboration.
2. A proposal is usually _______ in tone, as the audience is often a person or group in a position of authority.
👉 提案的语气应该是什么样的？
Tone — Formal（语气：正式） — 读者通常是有权威的人，因此语气需正式、礼貌、客观。
e.g. "It is recommended that..." rather than "I think you should..."
3. The _______ is commonly used in proposals. It makes the text sound more objective.
👉 哪种语态能让提案听起来更客观？
Passive voice（被动语态） — 让提案的语气更客观、更正式。主动语态也可以，但较少见。
e.g. "A photo-sharing space could be set up on the school server" rather than "We could set up a photo-sharing space."
4. To propose something, the _______ such as the future simple, future continuous, or the second conditional (could, would) are used.
👉 提案中应该多用哪种时态？
Tense — Future & Conditional（时态：将来时与条件式） — 多用将来时和 could/would 来表达建议与可能性。
e.g. This would increase membership. Students could upload their photos. The club would benefit.

Part Two · Model Example

📝 Proposal 示例：Music Streaming

对照格式表格，逐段分析这篇范文——哪个是 Introduction？哪个是 Body？Closing remark 用了什么句型？

To/From/SubjectTo: Mr Raymond Chan, Head of Marketing
From: Chris Wong, Student Representative
Proposal: Ways to boost "Music is Live" subscribers

IntroductionThe purpose of this proposal is to suggest ways to increase the number of subscribers to the "Music is Live" streaming service. Currently, the service has 50,000 subscribers, but there is potential for significant growth.

Body 11. Offer discounted rates for students
Many potential subscribers are students who cannot afford the full subscription fee. By offering a 50% discount for full-time students, we could attract a large number of new subscribers. This would also build brand loyalty among young people.

Body 22. Provide exclusive content
If we could secure exclusive live performances from popular local artists, subscribers would have a strong incentive to join and remain. Exclusive content is one of the most effective ways to differentiate our service from competitors.

ConclusionTo conclude, these two measures — student discounts and exclusive content — would significantly boost subscriber numbers. They are cost-effective and would strengthen our market position.

ClosingI hope these suggestions are useful and will be taken into consideration. I would be happy to discuss them further if needed.
📝 关键句型："The purpose of this proposal is to..." / "By + V-ing, we could..." / "If we could..., ... would have a strong incentive to..." / "To conclude, these measures would significantly..."

Q1: Which is NOT a feature of a leaflet's format?

✅ Formal — 提案是正式文件，读者通常是有权威的人，应该用 formal tone。

✅ To/From/Subject / Introduction / Body / Conclusion / Closing — 这是 Proposal 的完整格式结构。
Q4: Which persuasive technique is commonly used in leaflets?
✅ Rhetorical questions — 反问句是传单中常用的说服技巧。另外 Imperatives 和 you/we approach 也是常见手法。

✅ Arguments for both sides — Discussion essay 必须考虑正方和反方观点，然后给出自己的结论。
