转折阶段Fortunately/Unfortunately, ... / A turning point came when ... / It was not until ... that ... / However, things began to change when ...
结尾阶段In the end, ... / Eventually, ... / As a result, ... / Today, ...

📝 范文：Depression: More Than Just Feeling Sad

Task: Write an article for the school magazine about your friend's depression and how he/she overcame it. Give advice to others on how to help fight depression.

Depression: More Than Just Feeling Sad

Have you ever felt overwhelmed by stress or noticed a friend becoming unusually quiet and withdrawn? Have you ever wondered whether they were simply stressed or facing something more serious? These questions became very real to me when one of my close friends suffered from depression. His experience taught me valuable lessons about mental health and the importance of supporting those who are struggling.

My friend, Jason, was once an energetic and cheerful student who enjoyed participating in school activities. He was always eager to join class events, spend time with friends and take on new challenges with enthusiasm. However, everything began to change when he entered senior secondary school. The pressure of examinations, high expectations from his parents and a growing lack of confidence gradually took a toll on him.

At first, Jason often seemed unusually down and worried. As time went by, these negative emotions began to affect his behaviour. He stopped joining extracurricular activities, rarely met up with friends and gradually lost interest in the hobbies he once enjoyed. Before long, his condition started to affect him physically as well. He often fell exhausted, had difficulty concentrating in class and struggled to sleep at night. Like many teenagers, Jason believed that he could handle the problem on his own and did not seek help. Unfortunately, his condition continued to worsen. His academic performance declined significantly, and he increasingly isolated himself from those around him.

Jason realised that he could not overcome the problem on his own and decided to seek professional help. He began attending counselling sessions, where he learned effective ways to manage stress and cope with negative emotions. He also started exercising regularly and learned to open up about his feelings instead of keeping everything bottled up inside. Throughout this difficult journey, his family, teachers and friends stood by his side and offered continuous support and encouragement whenever he needed it. Although recovery was not immediate, their support, together with his own efforts, helped him gradually regain his confidence and optimism. Today, he is much happier and more positive about life.

Jason's experience reminds us that depression can affect anyone. If you or someone you know is struggling with depression, do not ignore the warning signs. When feelings of hopelessness, anxiety or self-doubt arise, talk to trusted friends or family members instead of keeping everything to yourself. If you begin to withdraw from social activities or lose interest in your hobbies, try to stay connected with others and gradually return to your normal routines. Furthermore, if depression affects your physical well-being through fatigue, poor concentration or sleep problems, it is important to seek professional help from a psychologist or counsellor. At the same time, maintaining a healthy lifestyle through regular exercise can support both physical and mental recovery. By taking these steps early, people can prevent their condition from worsening and reduce its impact on their studies and daily lives.

If you have a friend who is suffering from depression, remember that your support can make a real difference. When they express negative emotions, listen patiently and show empathy rather than judgement. If they begin to withdraw from social activities or avoid friends, stay connected with them and encourage them to take part in daily activities. When depression starts to affect their physical well-being, encourage them to seek professional help. Your care and support can help them take the first step towards recovery.

Jason's story shows us that depression is far more than simply feeling sad. It is a serious mental health condition that can affect many aspects of a person's life. This experience taught me the importance of recognising the warning signs and offering support to those who are struggling. Let us pay closer attention to the people around us and remember that a simple conversation, a listening ear or a small act of kindness may make a life-changing difference to someone battling depression.
