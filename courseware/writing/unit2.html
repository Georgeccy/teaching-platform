Part 2 · Understanding the question

📋 审题：提取关键信息

📝 Task

Write about 400 words for the following topic:

You are doing a Bachelor of Education degree course in Secondary Education and you have been given the following essay question to answer as an assignment:

Most secondary school students in Hong Kong take part in at least four to five extra-curricular activities. What are the pros and cons of extra-curricular activities? Discuss.
  • Write a two-sided argumentative essay.
  • Discuss the pros and cons of students taking up extra-curricular activities.
  • Give your essay a title.
1. Role（你是谁）
a student doing a Bachelor of Education degree course in Secondary Education
2. Audience（写给谁）
lecturer / professor / course instructor
3. Purpose（写作目的，可多选）
✅ 前三项（benefits / disadvantages / reach a reasoned conclusion）是题目明确要求。第一项是陷阱——题目问的是 pros & cons，并非"是否应该参加"。
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ formal — 这是提交给大学讲师的课程作业，语气需正式、客观。
5. Tone（语气，可多选）
✅ 议论文语气应 logical + objective/balanced + persuasive。neutral 与 persuasive 略冲突；vivid and descriptive 属于记叙文/描写文，不适合议论文。
虽然题目没有明确要求学生给出自己的观点，但建议最后仍给一个结论（如"何种情况下才是有利的"）。议论文通常不是单纯列举优缺点，而是要做出分析与判断。

Part 3 · Brainstorming

🧠 想论点：从"人类需求"出发

思考 3.1：你参加过课外活动吗？若有，介绍一项你感兴趣 / 不喜欢的；若无，说明不参加的理由。
开放性问题，旨在结合学生自身经历，更容易联想到利弊观点。例如：喜欢——释放压力、感受乐趣、锻炼身体；不喜欢——太累、家长要求参加；不参加——没时间、没精力。
思考 3.2：参加课外活动除了培养技能，是否也能培养某种价值观？举例说明。
可以。如团队性体育活动培养协作意识、抗压能力；志愿活动培养责任感与同理心。
思考 3.3：从个人理想与未来发展看，参加课外活动能带来什么好处？
探索并发现自己的兴趣与特长，有利于未来的职业选择与发展。
思考 3.4：题目提到 at least four to five，是否意味着可能过度参与？会带来什么问题？
会。压力大、不利于心理健康；难以平衡学习与课后活动，影响成绩。
思考 3.5：有的父母认为某些课外班能培养技能，便擅自给孩子报班。这会带来什么问题？
忽略孩子的兴趣与意愿，造成孩子压力，违背了课外活动的初衷。

Appendix · Model Answer

📝 范文：Benefit or Burden

Task: Write a two-sided argumentative essay (~400 words) on the pros and cons of extra-curricular activities.

Benefit or Burden: The Role of Extra-curricular Activities

It is quite common for secondary school students in Hong Kong these days to take part in several extra-curricular activities. While some people believe these activities are beneficial because they help students develop important skills and interests, others argue that they may place unnecessary pressure on students. This essay will discuss the advantages and disadvantages of taking part in extra-curricular activities.

One major advantage of extra-curricular activities is that they help students develop valuable skills and positive personal qualities. While school lessons mainly focus on academic knowledge, many activities provide opportunities for students to practise teamwork, leadership, communication and problem-solving skills. For example, members of sports teams or competition clubs often learn how to cooperate with others and perform under pressure. In addition, activities such as volunteer service or charity events can help students become more responsible and develop greater empathy for people in need. As a result, students not only gain practical skills but also develop important values and qualities that can benefit them throughout their lives.

Another benefit is that extra-curricular activities allow students to explore their interests and discover their aptitudes. By participating in different clubs and activities, students may find subjects or fields that they genuinely enjoy or naturally good at. For example, a student who joins a science club may develop a strong interest in scientific research, while a student who participates in drama activities may discover a talent for public speaking. Understanding their interests and aptitudes can help students stay motivated in their studies and make better decisions about their future career paths. Therefore, these activities can play an important role in students' long-term development.

However, taking part in too many extra-curricular activities can create significant pressure for students. As students progress to higher forms, they often face heavier workloads and more demanding examinations. If they spend too much time on activities, they may have less time for rest and relaxation. Over time, this may lead to stress, exhaustion and even burnout, which can negatively affect their mental wellbeing. In addition, students who are constantly busy may struggle to concentrate in class or complete assignments on time. As a result, their academic performance may gradually decline. Therefore, joining too many activities can sometimes do more harm than good.

Another disadvantage of extra-curricular activities is that some students are pressured by their parents to participate in activities that do not reflect their genuine interests. In Hong Kong, many parents believe that learning a wide range of skills can enhance their children's competitiveness and future career prospects. As a result, students may be required to attend numerous classes, such as music lessons, language courses and sports training, regardless of their personal preferences. This lack of choice can reduce students' motivation and enjoyment of learning. More importantly, it may prevent them from exploring their own talents and developing a strong sense of identity. Consequently, extra-curricular activities may become a source of stress rather than an opportunity for personal growth.

In conclusion, extra-curricular activities have both advantages and disadvantages. On the one hand, they help students develop useful skills, positive values and a better understanding of their interests and strengths. On the other hand, excessive participation and a lack of freedom in choosing activities may create unnecessary pressure and reduce the benefits students receive. Ultimately, extra-curricular activities are most beneficial when students are able to choose activities that genuinely interest them and participate in a balanced and manageable way.
