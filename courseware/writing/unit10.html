Task 2A · Understanding the Question

📋 审题分析

Your school is holding a Global Food Festival. You have been asked to write an introduction to the festival. Write the introduction.

GLOBAL FOOD FESTIVAL · INTRODUCTION
• About the Global Food Festival
• Activities you can take part in
• Dishes you can try
1. Role（你是谁）
The writer introducing the festival / Yourself
2. Audience（写给谁）
Students, teachers and visitors interested in the festival
3. Purpose（写作目的）
To introduce the Global Food Festival and encourage people to take part in it
4. Register（正式程度）
✅ semi-formal — 校园活动简介面向师生和访客，语气正式但不刻板
5. Tone（语气，可多选）— 勾选后点击「检查答案」
✅ 应选：Informative / Engaging / Positive。Introduction 需要提供信息的同时吸引读者参与，语气积极正面。不选 Objective — 宣传文不是客观报告。

Part 5 · Think 5.1

🤔 A/B 对比：哪个更适合 Introduction？

📍思考5.1: 对比AB两个活动介绍的版本，为什么A更适合introduction？
B. 通知式

The Global Food Festival is held / will be held at Kowloon East Memorial School on 12th March.

A. 介绍型 ✅

The Global Food Festival is a special event held at Kowloon East Memorial School on 12th March.

因为 B 写法更适合《通知》，是直接提供活动的信息。而写 introduction 简介 / promotional texts 宣传文本的时候，是需要用 A 写法"介绍型"句子的，即需要定义"这是一个什么样的活动"：a XX event.
📌 Notes: 对于已经确定的未来活动，is held 和 will be held 都可以。
• is held 较客观、简介式（常见于 introduction/leaflet）
• will be held 更强调未来即将发生的活动。

Language Bank · 活动总介绍型句式

🔘 Language Bank — 活动总介绍型句式

Sentence 1 — 定义活动

Activity Name + is a/an + adjective + event + held + 地点 + 时间

e.g. The Global Food Festival is a special event held at Kowloon East Memorial School on March 15th.

Sentence 2 — 介绍体验

During this + adjective + event/week/day, participants can + do A + and + do B, V-ing + result/benefit.

e.g. During this exciting week, students can take part in a variety of fun activities and enjoy delicious dishes, discovering different cultures through food.

📌 Notes:
(1) 关于分词 done/doing 的用法：
• held + 地点/时间是一种更简洁的表达方式，相当于省略了 which is held...
• V-ing + 收获/结果用来补充活动带来的体验、意义或收获，使句子更紧凑、更生动。

(2) 当介绍活动的基本资讯时，通常使用 students / participants / visitors 等较客观的人称。当直接邀请读者参与活动时，则更常使用第二人称 you。

Appendix · Model Answer

📝 范文：Global Food Festival Introduction

Task: Write an introduction to the Global Food Festival. (~285 words, 3 paragraphs)

Global Food Festival — Introduction

Get ready for a week full of flavor, culture and excitement! The Global Food Festival is a special event held at Kowloon East Memorial School on March 15th. During this exciting week, students can take part in a variety of fun activities and enjoy delicious dishes from around the world, discovering different cultures through food. It is truly a week of discovery, enjoyment and celebration. Come and experience a global journey right here on campus!

A wide range of fun and interactive activities will take place during lunchtime and after school. One of the highlights is the hands-on cooking classes, where you can learn how to prepare some of the world's most delicious dishes. Popular food films and documentaries will also be shown, helping you understand how food connects people and cultures. There will also be interactive games and quizzes for you to explore different food traditions in an enjoyable way. Try out new skills, experience new tastes and explore cultures through these engaging activities. Whether you love cooking or simply want to try something new, there is something for everyone.

Of course, no food festival would be complete without the food itself — and each day will take you on a journey across a different continent. Start your week with North American favorites like juicy burgers and classic tacos. Then travel to South America to enjoy smoky Brazilian-style barbecue. On Wednesday, explore African flavors with dishes such as grilled kebabs and boiled plantains. Thursday brings European classics, including Spanish seafood rice, spaghetti and fish and chips. Finally, end the week with Asian Food Day, featuring Korean kimchi, Japanese sushi and Thai delights like tom yum soup and mango sticky rice. From North America to Asia, discover a world of flavors without leaving our school!
