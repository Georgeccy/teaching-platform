Task Analysis

📋 审题：Letter of Advice 考什么？

Your friend Susan has sent you a letter saying that she is very worried about her exams. She studies until midnight every night. She feels depressed because her grades are dropping, even though she is working hard. Write a letter to your friend giving her advice.
1. Role（你的身份）——你是谁？
A friend of Susan（Susan 的朋友）— 语气要亲切、支持、有同理心
2. Audience（读者）——写给谁看？
Susan（你的朋友）— 因此语气要 sympathetic、positive、encouraging
3. Purpose（写作目的）——为什么写？
To give advice on how to deal with exam stress and improve study habits（就如何应对考试压力、改善学习习惯给出建议）
4. Register（语域）——用什么语气？
✅ semi-formal / informal — 写给朋友的建议信，语气亲切自然，像朋友聊天

Four Principles

💝 建议信的四大写作原则

① Be sympathetic 有同理心

Show sympathy for your reader's problem(s).
例：I'm very sorry to hear that you're feeling depressed and tired.

② Be positive 保持积极

Give positive comments and useful suggestions.
例：I'd like to give you some suggestions that might help.

③ Support the reader 支持读者

Offer advice to a friend — he or she will appreciate your support.
例：I also get stressed because of my grades, so I can understand how you feel.

④ Offer encouragement 给予鼓励

Encourage your reader to be hopeful and be positive.
例：I'm sure things will improve soon!

📝 核心心法：建议信 = 同理心开场 + 具体建议 + 积极结尾。永远让读者感到被理解、被支持。

Part Three · Two-Sided Argument

📐 什么是 Two-Sided Argument？

🤔先想一想：下面哪段话更符合双面议论文的特点？点击你的选择！
✅ B 是正确的！双面议论文需要呈现 both sides（正反两面），保持 balance（平衡），尊重不同观点。
📖 定义：A two-sided argument must present the different sides of an issue and can put forward an opinion. It should show different points of view so that it is objective.

Part Three · Two Approaches

📊 Objective vs Subjective：两种写法

Objective 客观写法

present both sides and write objectively
不表明个人立场，平衡呈现正反两面

Subjective 主观写法

make your point of view clear from the start
从一开始就明确表明立场，但仍尊重对方观点

写法特点适用场景
Objective平衡呈现，不下结论要求"讨论正反两面"的题目
Subjective明确立场，理性反驳要求"表达个人观点"的题目
⚠️ 重要提醒：If you choose to make your point of view clear, be sure to respect other points of view. You can still challenge them, and this is common in debates.

Part Three · Language Choices

📐 双面议论文的四大语言技巧

① Have balance 保持平衡

On the one hand... / On the other hand...
Some people believe that... / Some people argue that...

② Use rhetorical questions 反问

Do tired students perform well at school?
Is being underweight healthy?
注意：不要过多使用

③ Pay attention to tone 注意语气

Sadly, / Worryingly, / Shockingly,
表达 concerned / troubled / shocked

④ Use strong verbs 强动词

Many people claim... (stronger than "say")
Our health is vital. (stronger than "important")

Part Three · Analysis

📝 辩论稿分析：Objective 还是 Subjective？

阅读以下辩论稿片段，判断它是客观还是主观写法。

Title: 'The best way to keep fit is to go on a diet?'

Introduction: People living in cities are often unfit. Some of them believe that the best way to get fit is to go on a diet. There are, however, other better and healthier ways through which to get fit.

Body: Dieting usually means eating less. Fitness means being healthy and strong... To exercise, people need energy from food... Too much exercise can be unhealthy...

Conclusion: To conclude, dieting to get fit is unwise. Instead, people need to make sure that they do not over- or under-eat. In addition, regular exercise should be accompanied by a healthy diet.
这篇辩论稿是 Objective 还是 Subjective？
✅ Subjective — 作者在 Introduction 就表明立场（"There are, however, other better and healthier ways"），Body 主要论证反方观点，Conclusion 明确说 "dieting to get fit is unwise"。虽然提到了对方观点，但整体是为主观立场服务的。

Part Three · Common Mistakes

⚠️ 双面议论文常见错误

点击卡片查看解析 →

错误 1：这句话哪里错了？

Some people think dieting is good. But I think they are all wrong and stupid. Only exercise works.

💡 点击卡片查看解析

✅ 解析

问题：语气不礼貌，攻击对方。双面议论文必须尊重不同观点，即使要反驳也要理性。

✓ While some people believe that dieting is effective, I would argue that exercise is more important for long-term health.

错误 2：这句话哪里错了？

Do you think dieting is good? Do you think exercise is good? Do you think health is important? Do you think we should care? Of course we should!

💡 点击卡片查看解析

✅ 解析

问题：反问句过多，显得啰嗦且不够专业。rhetorical questions 应该适度使用，增强说服力而非堆砌。

✓ Do tired students perform well at school? Of course not. This shows why adequate sleep is essential for academic success.
