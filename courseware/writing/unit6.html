Section 9 · Rebuttal Logic

🤔 思考9.1 + 反驳方法

思考9.1：将以下5个步骤拖拽排列成正确顺序（拖拽条目调整位置）

反驳反方的两种常用方法：

方法1：降低反方重要性
  • 对方影响有限/对方也有问题
  • 对方问题可以解决
  • 非必须

举例：children need to play → 对方也有问题 (limited to a particular age group, require maintenance) → 非必须 (does not necessarily mean developing more playgrounds)

方法2：与我方对比
  • 我方好处更多/影响更大

举例：green spaces → benefit people of different age groups → require less maintenance

📝 范文 Debate Speech

Task: Write a debate speech arguing that green spaces should be developed more than children's playgrounds.

Debate Speech: Green Spaces vs Children's Playgrounds

Good afternoon, everyone,

The topic before us today is how limited urban land should be used. In light of the evidence discussed, our team firmly believes that green spaces should be developed more than children's playgrounds. They protect our health, bring people together and make our city more sustainable.

First, green spaces are essential to public health. In a fast-paced city like Hong Kong, many of us study or work for long hours and face constant pressure. Green spaces can help relieve this pressure through the well-known "twenty-minute park effect". Residents can take a short walk under the trees, look at flowers and plants, or simply enjoy the natural surroundings in a nearby park. Spending around twenty minutes in a green environment can help lower stress and calm the mind. As a result, regular contact with nature can improve our physical health and mental well-being.

Second, green spaces help strengthen family and community relationships. In many neighbourhoods, people may live close together, but they do not really know their neighbours. Green spaces give people a shared place to meet, relax and interact naturally. For example, parents can spend meaningful time with their children, teenagers can chat with friends after school, and elderly residents can sit, exercise and talk to neighbours. When people of different ages regularly share the same space, they gradually become familiar with one another and develop trust. In this way, green spaces can bring neighbours closer and help people feel that they belong to the same community.

Third, green spaces make our city healthier and more sustainable. This is because they help improve the urban environment and reduce the impact of environmental problems on our daily lives. Trees and plants can filter dust and pollutants from the air, so residents can enjoy a cleaner living environment. They also provide shade and help cool down streets during hot summer days. During heavy rain, green spaces can absorb rainwater, reducing pressure on drainage systems and lowering the risk of flooding. These benefits may not seem obvious at first, but they matter to every resident because they help create a cleaner, cooler and safer city for all of us.

Some people may argue that children's playgrounds are important because children need places to play and exercise outdoors. This is a fair point because outdoor play can help children stay active and develop social skills. However, this does not mean that playgrounds should be developed more than green spaces. When land in Hong Kong is so limited, it is only fair to choose the option that benefits the whole community. Children's playgrounds mainly serve young children and parents, and they are mainly designed for play. They also require regular maintenance and supervision to remain safe. Green spaces are different. They can be used by children, teenagers, adults and the elderly in different ways. They are more flexible, more inclusive and more beneficial to the community as a whole.

To conclude, this debate is about making the best use of our limited urban land. A playground may serve one group well, but a green space can serve all of us. It protects our health, connects our community and improves our environment. That is why we strongly believe that green spaces should be developed more than children's playgrounds.
