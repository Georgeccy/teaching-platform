Part A · Model Essay
My Favourite Teacher — Chris Wong (S.3A)

I still remember the day I was sitting alone in the school playground sobbing over the fact that my team had lost another inter-school football match. Being the captain of the school football team, I could not forgive myself for leading my teammates into a downward spiral of failure and shame. As I was burying my face in my palms, our PE coach Mr Tam walked towards me. I looked up with my puffy, bloodshot eyes as he gently placed his hand on my shoulder while tears were streaming down my face. Instantly, he knew why I was there and told me that although winning games is, of course, every athlete's ultimate desire, one should never feel depressed over failures.

Mr Tam explained that I had to learn from my miscalculations in our previous games in order to improve the team and hone my skills as a footballer. He also told me that my teammates all had faith in me despite the serious setbacks they had faced. 'Remember, Chris. It doesn't matter how many times you get knocked down, but how many times you get back up.' His words immediately calmed the storm in my mind and gave me a substantial boost, which allowed me to move on.

From that day on, I stayed after school to practise my football moves with extra motivation and passion. Whenever I feel the past is haunting me, I simply think of Mr Tam's words. Had it not been for his guidance, I would never have been able to keep my chin up and become this year's recipient of the 'Sportsman of the Year' award. That is why Mr Tam is my favourite teacher.

💡 技能标注

具体场景开头："I still remember the day..." — 画面感强
情绪递进：sobbing → could not forgive myself → tears streaming → calmed the storm → keep my chin up
引语：直接引用老师的原话，增加真实感
倒装句式："Had it not been for..." — 高级语法加分

Part B · Q9 Model Essay
Email about Spending — Chris to Peter

Subject: Advice on spending
Dear Peter,

I am glad that you were willing to share your recent worries with me. Please don't see it as unhelpful, or some type of betrayal, when I turn down your request to borrow money. I firmly believe that it is best not to wrestle with the issue and that instead we opt for remedial measures and positive solutions promptly.

As a close friend of yours, I must say that I am very worried about your spending patterns and current finances. With us being students, we should be focusing on our education instead of concerning ourselves with style and high-end fashion. I understand that the sneakers and fragrance you have started wearing recently may give you enormous self-confidence and satisfy your vanity. Yet resorting to materialism like this simply opens doors not only to monetary but also psychological issues. Such behaviour is nothing but a magnet for anxiety and nightmarish experiences.

Do you remember that time when you were so obsessed with squandering a fortune on branded fashion goods that you only had enough money for one meal a day? You told me that it was reasonable to suffer in pursuit of a dream wardrobe. Clearly, in the light of day, this is by no means justifiable! There is much more to life than exorbitantly priced designer fashion. You are in fact jeopardizing your health at a time when you need to be well nourished, as you train with the school swimming team during puberty.

Part B · Q9 Model Essay (续)

Given your spiralling condition, I am compelled to give you advice on rectifying this issue, with the first solution being investing in basic clothing and trends that stay. This will be particularly beneficial to your wallet as there will be no need to revamp your wardrobe constantly. Mixing and matching plain T-shirts and jeans can be chic and stylish enough!

In addition, try making a spending log to record all your daily expenditure. Use a small notebook or one of those managing apps on your phone. Jot down every expense made throughout the day and thereby grasp an immediate understanding of how to improve your spending patterns. Find out what costs you the most money and what trivial things you can sacrifice on a daily basis.

Also, please tidy up your closet. By tidying, I don't mean simply putting things in order but digging deep at the back of the wardrobe. You may find some forgotten but interesting shirts or jackets to wear, deterring you from plunging into more fashion debt.

At the end of the day, all this hinges on you making some changes. But this is definitely not the end of the world. Remember, I am always here to support you. Your new regime may prove tough at first but once underway I promise that all your efforts will be worthwhile.

Best,
Chris

💡 技能标注

反问回忆："Do you remember that time when..." — 用具体事例说服
递进建议：三条建议层层递进（投资基础款 → 做消费记录 → 整理衣柜）
高级词汇：resort to materialism / spiralling condition / be compelled to / hinge on
