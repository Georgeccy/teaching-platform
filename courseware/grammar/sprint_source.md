## 单元1：课程介绍


### 1.1 课程必要性

- **无英语环境** — 母语者通过观察和模仿掌握语法 非母语者通过学习和实践掌握语法
- **学习时间短** — 母语者从出生起就开始潜移默化的学习； 非母语者面临着短期内出分的学习压力，不太可能自己一点点总结规律

### 1.2 课程目的

- 自己写的句子别人能看懂
- 别人写的句子自己能看懂

### 1.3 课程形式

- 讲一下知识点，做一下翻译
- 讲完了一群知识点，集中做一会儿翻译

### 1.4 学习路线


#### 先学习基本句型

- 定义：一个句子的每个成分都不能省略，否则就是病句。

##### 种类：根据所用的主要动词不同（及物动词、不及物动词、系动词）可分为五类：

- 主语+谓语（不及物动词）
- 主语+谓语（及物动词）+宾语
- 主语+谓语（及物动词）+间接宾语+直接宾语
- 主语+谓语（及物动词）+宾语+宾语补足语

###### 主语+系动词+表语

- 还有一种不及上述五种结构普通的结构：主语+谓语动词+主语补足语，如He died young

#### 再学习单句的组合

- 并列句、名词性从句、状语从句、定语从句

## 单元2：基本句型


### 2.1 基本句型1：主+谓+宾


#### 🌟 例句：示范例句

- **他讲英文。**
  - He speaks English.
- **这些小孩踢了足球。**
  - These children played football.
- **家长们减少了孩子们的屏幕时间**  _screen time_
  - Parents reduced (their) children’s screen time或the screen time of their children/kids.

#### 如果主语也是个动作?

- **踢足球可以改善健康。**
  - ❌ Play football can improve health ❌
  - 💡 这时需使用动名词（即动词+ing，使它具有名词的性质）
  - ✅ Playing football can improve health.
- **讲英文会提高工作前景。**  _job prospects_
  - ❌ Speak English can improve job prospects❌
  - 💡 这时需使用动名词（即动词+ing，使它具有名词的性质）
  - ✅ Speaking English can improve job prospects.

##### 动名词变化规则

- **一般情况下: 直接在动词后加ing** — 如:eat——eating、study——studying

###### 特殊变形:

- **以e结尾的动词(e不发音):去e加ing** — 如:make——making、breathe——breathing
- **以ie结尾的动词:变ie为y，再加ing** — 如:lie——lying、die——dying
- **重读闭音节:双写末尾字母，加ing** — 如:cut——cutting、sip——sipping

#### 如果宾语也是个动作？

> enjoy, like, dislike, consider, stop, hate, practice

- **我喜欢每周都去看个电影。**
  - I enjoy/like going to the cinema every week.
  - I like watching/seeing a movie/film every week
- **很多学生讨厌写作业。**
  - Many students hate to do/doing homework.

### 2.3 基本句型3：主+谓+宾+宾补

- **例句**：例句：The funding / enables / the company / to expand.（这笔资金使该公司得以扩张。）

#### 为什么会有宾语补足语？

- 因为有些及物动词充当谓语时，只靠主谓宾的句子结构无法完整表达意思（意思是残缺的），需要在宾语后面加上一些词或词组进行补充说明。

#### 写宾补：名词性和形容词性的词经常作为宾语补足语

> 主谓宾的要点同前，故不重复


##### 补语是动词原形

> 即 不带to的不定式

- make/let/help + somebody + to do sth

##### 补语是to+动词原形

> 即 不定式

- enable/allow/encourage/push/force/prompt/inspire/motivate/teach/ask/want/invite + somebody + to do sth

##### 补语是from+v-ing

> 即 介词短语

- discourage/prevent/stop/prohibit/forbid/ban somebody + from doing sth

##### 补语是v-ing

> 即 现在分词

- see/hear somebody + doing something

##### 补语是形容词

- find/consider/make/keep + sb/sth + adj

#### 🌟 例句：主谓宾宾补翻译（7句）

- **老师们应该阻拦学生们吸烟。(discourage)**
  - ✅Teachers should stop/prevent/discourage students from smoking.
- **政府应该阻止这些意外发生。（prevent）**
  - ✅The government should prevent these accidents from happening/taking place
  - 也可以说avoid these accidents from happening，但这个说法 比较少用。
- **父母不应该强迫孩子学习数学（force）**
  - ✅Parents should not force children into learning maths
  - ✅Parents should not force children to learn maths.
  - math, mathematics都可)
- **不景气的乡村经济致使很多人搬到城市(the sluggish rural economy)**
  - ✅The sluggish rural economy caused many people to move/relocate to cities.
  - ✅ The sluggish rural economy made many people move/relocate to cities.
  - ✅ Because of the sluggish rural economy, many people moved to cities.
  - ❌ ❌ The sluggish rural economy let many people move/relocate to cities. 这里 let 虽然也翻译为“让”，但一般表示的是“允许”，而不是“导致”
- **教育使学生成为富有学识的工作者（knowledgeable workers）**
  - ✅Education enables students to become knowledgeable workers.
  - ✅Because of education, students can become knowledgeable workers.
  - ✅Education makes students knowledgable workers.
  - ❌ ❌Education makes students become knowledgable workers. 根据表达习惯，这里的become是多余的
- **网课使学生能够按照自己的步调学习（online courses; at their own pace）**
  - ✅Taking/attending/doing/having online courses enables students to study at their own pace.
  - ✅If students attend online courses, they can study at their own pace.
  - ✅Students who take online courses can study at their own pace.
  - ❌ ❌Online courses make students can study at their own pace.因为情态动词只能构成谓语，不能用于宾补
- **沉重的学业任务使得很多学生不能够发展兴趣爱好(heavy academic tasks, interests and hobbies)**
  - ✅ Because of heavy academic tasks, many students cannot develop interests and hobbies.
  - ✅ Heavy academic tasks prevent/stop/discourage many students from developing interests and hobbies.
  - ✅ Heavy academic tasks make it difficult/ impossible for many students to develop interests and hobbies.
  - ✅ Heavy academic tasks mean that many students cannot develop interests and hobbies.
  - ✅ Academic tasks are so heavy that many students have no time to develop interests and hobbies.
  - ❓ Heavy academic tasks make many students not develop interests and hobbies.
  - ❌ ❌ Heavy academic tasks make many students cannot develop interests and hobbies.
  - ❌ ❌Academic tasks are too heavy for students to develop interests and hobbies.

### 2.4 基本句型4：主+谓+双宾

- **例句**：例句：这家公司/将会提供给/学生们/很多暑期实习 (offer; summer internships)
  - This company/ will offer/ students/ many summer internships.
  - This company will offer/ many summer internships/ to students.
  - 对比The media/ have made/ sports stars/ opinion leaders（主谓宾宾补）

#### 为何会有两个宾语：

- 因为有些动词不可避免会涉及两个对象
- 比如bring, give, lend, offer, show, teach, tell, ask, send, buy

#### 这类句型常有两种写法：

> 其中人是间接宾语，物是直接宾语

- **写法1～主+谓+sb+sth** — 推荐写法
- **写法2～主+谓+sth+to/for+sb** — 由于表达习惯，部分动词不能用该写法

##### 主谓双宾 vs 主谓宾宾补


###### 成分位置是否可变

- 主谓双宾：两个宾语的位置常常可以调换（即写法1和写法2互换）；
- 主谓宾宾补：宾补基本只在宾语后面

###### 成分是否只能名词

- 主谓双宾：两个宾语都只能是名词性成分
- 主谓宾宾补：宾补除了名词外还可以是形容词性成分

#### 🌟 例句：主谓双宾翻译（6句）

- **转基因科技将会给人们更多的选择（GM technology）**
  - Transgenic/GM technology will give/offer people more choices/options.
- **政府已经提供给一些城市很多资金，去保护历史建筑（historic buildings）**
  - The government has offered many cities a large amount of funds/funding to preserve/protect historic buildings.
- **平板电脑的发明已经给我们省去了很多麻烦(tablet; save sb trouble)**
  - The invention of tablets/the tablet has saved us a lot of trouble
- **这家机构颁发给了我一份特别的奖学金(award; scholarship)**
  - ✅This institution awarded me a special scholarship
  - ✅This institution awarded a special scholarship to me.
- **这位交警罚了这位醉驾者很多钱（traffic police officer; fine; drink driver）**
  - The traffic police officer fined this drink driver a large amount of money(由于表达习惯，此处不可调换双宾语位置)
- **这位老师给我们出了一道难题（set; a difficult problem）**
  - The teacher set us a difficult problem (由于表达习惯，此处不可调换双宾语位置)

### 2.5 基本句型5：主+系+表


#### 系动词

- 连接主语和表语的动词
- 无被动语态，大多也没有进行时态

##### 常用系动词


###### 表示延续:

- be动词 (am，is，are), continue, stay, keep, remain, stand等

###### 表示变化:

- get, grow, become, turn, go, fall, prove 等

###### 表示感官:

- 视觉：look, seem, appear
- 感觉/触觉：feel
- 听觉：sound
- 味觉：taste, eat
- 嗅觉：smell等

#### 表语

- 也称主语补语，是说明主语的身份、性质、品性、特征和状态的词或从句

##### 可以由形容词性成分充当(形容词、分词、介宾短语)

- The book is interesting. 主补（表语）

##### 可以由名词性成分充当(名词、代词、不定式、动名词)

- I am a student.

##### *定语和补语的区别?


###### 定语属于修饰语，是可以省略的（省略后对句子结构没有影响）

- I like reading interesting books.

###### 补语是基本句型一部分，不可以省略（不要误以为带“补”字就能省略）

- He found the book interesting.（宾语补足语）
- The book is interesting.（表语，即主语补足语）

#### 🌟 例句：常用系动词相关翻译

- **我们的下一步是得到资金。（funding）**
  - Our next step is to get funding/funds.
- **旅游业似乎是一个经济增长的驱动力(a driver of economic growth)。**
  - The tourist industry/The tourism industry/Tourism seemed(to be)/appeared(to be) a driving force/a driver of economic growth.
- **针对女性的数字保持在60%不变。（the figure for sb）**
  - The figure for women remained unchanged/constant at 60%
  - ...stood at 60%
  - ...stabilized at 60%
  - ...leveled off/out at 60%
- **警官们的存在成为了一个威慑物。（presence；deterrent威慑物）**
  - The presence of police officers became a deterrent.
- **这条建议证明是很有用的。**
  - This suggestion/piece of advice proves(to be) useful.
  - This suggestion/piece of advice was proved useful (by experts)

### 2.6 It充当形式主语和形式宾语


#### It充当形式主语


##### 为何要用？

- 为了正确书写【某人做某事很难、很重要】这类句型

###### 🌟 例句：例句翻译：政府很难改变人们的行为。

- **改错 ：The government changed people's behavior was difficult.**
  - 💡 错误原因：将句子作为主语

### 2.7 There be 句型


#### 何时用there be句型?


##### 想要强调某人/某物是存在的。

- There was
- There is
- There have/has been
- There will be

#### 如何分析结构？

- 可以看作一个倒装结构。There 原本充当表语，在这个句型中被前置了。
- **例句**：例句：There are two boys in the room.
  - 房间里有两个男孩。
  - 可以看作Two boys are there in the room(虽然不会这么说)

##### There be 句型的主语是be后面的名词，要保持主谓一致

- **有大量的高层建筑在这个城市中。（a large number of大量；high-rise building高层建筑）**
  - There are a large number of high-rise buildings in this city.

##### There be句型中不能再出现独立句子

- **有各种因素导致了这个问题（factor 因素；contribute to导致）**
  - ✅ There were various factors which contributed to this problem.
  - ✅ There were various factors contributing to this problem.
  - ✅ Various factors contributed to this problem.
  - ❌ ❌ There were various factors contributed to this problem.

##### There be句型里的谓语动词一般只能是be动词（am/is/are/have been/has been）或再加上情态动词(should, might)，不能用其他动词


###### 改错：There will have two meetings tomorrow.

- 这里出现了及物动词have, 应该改为There will be two meetings tomorrow.
- 或者We will have two meetings tomorrow.
- 注意和含have been的用法区分：There have been two meetings since yesterday.

##### 为了简化句子，也可以去掉there be

- **原句：There were a great number of dead leaves lying on the ground** — 地上有很多枯叶

### 2.8 句型总结


#### 易错


##### 写主语&宾语

- ⚠️ 名词性的东西才能充当主语或者宾语，动词不能充当句子的主语或者宾语

###### ⚠️名词分为可数名词和不可数名词。可数名词往往不单独出现，要么前面加冠词/其他限定词，要么写成复数


###### ⚠️ 冠词用法


###### 名词前加什么冠词?


###### 看名词是否可数


###### 不可数


###### 一般是泛指，什么都不加

- **例句**：例句：I want advice.
  - 解析：泛指任何建议，不用定冠词。

###### 如果有限定成分或上下文，加the

- **例句**：例句：The advice that you gave me was useful.
  - 解析：advice后面出现了定语从句，属于限定成分。
- **例句**：例句：I thanked my brother for giving me advice. However, the advice may not be helpful to me.
  - 解析：前一句话说明了advice是我的brother给的，属于上下文。

###### 可数，再看是单数还是复数形式


###### 复数


###### 一般是泛指，什么都不加


###### We need to read books to be knowledgeable. (√)

- 解析：我们需要读书，不管什么书都可以，泛指。

###### 如果有限定成分或上下文，加the

- **例句**：例句：The books in this library have been damaged. (√)
  - 解析：in this library属于限定性成分
- **例句**：例句：Yesterday, my teacher mentioned The Tragedy of Hamlet and The Merchant of Venice. I want to read the/these books.
  - 双方都明确想读的书属于昨天老师提到的那些。

###### 单数


###### 一般是泛指，加a,an

- **例句**：例句：I want to buy a phone this summer.
  - 解析：关于手机没有任何限定性成分或上下文，属于泛指。
- **例句**：例句：I need to proofread an essay.
  - 解析：说话人要校对一篇特定的文章，但听话人却不知道具体是哪一篇文章，属于不定特指(简单理解成泛指也可)。

###### 如果有限定成分且能体现唯一性，才用the，否则仍用a/an

- **例句**：例句：Stiff punishment is an effective method to combat crime.
  - 解析：尽管method有限定性成分to combat crime，但能打击犯罪的方法并不只有一种，所以不是唯一的，仍然属于泛指。
- **例句**：例句：Tim will proofread an essay that he wrote last night.
  - 解析：由于说话人并不知道Tim昨晚写了多少篇文章（仅知道至少写了1篇），仍然属于泛指。
  - 区分：Tim will proofread one of the essays that he wrote last night.
- **例句**：例句：Tim will proofread the essay that he wrote last night.
  - 解析：由于说话人知道Tim昨晚只写了1篇文章，所以用the。

###### 如果有上下文，加the

- **例句**：例句：Tim will proofread an essay that he wrote last night. The essay was very interesting.
  - 解析：前一句话说明了这篇essay是Tim昨晚写的，属于上下文。

###### 如果该可数名词本身就是世上的唯一存在，也用the，表示独指

- **例句**：例句：He found a job in the tourist industry. (√)
  - 解析：这句话industry前面用the（因为一个国家一般只有一个旅游业，不可能有多个），而job前面用a（因为工作有很多个，现在只是说其中一个）。

###### 特殊情况：可数名词单数不加任何冠词。


###### 固定搭配

- **例句**：例句：She normally travels to work by train every day. (√)
- 解析：某些名词+介词是一个固定搭配，不需要加定冠词，譬如说by the train就是错的

###### 名词前已有其他限定词，比如形容词性物主代词和所有格

- **例句**：例句：I used her book last night. (√)
- 解析：名词如果前面有形容词性物主代词his, her, their, my等，不用加the
- **例句**：例句：I have been impressed by this new employee’s report. (√)
- 解析：名词前面如果有所有格，不用加the

###### ⚠️ 容易弄错“数”的限定词

- 如图

###### 单数：each, every等

- **每一位学生/都很用功**
  - Every/Each student works/studys hard

  - This factory wasted a lot of water.

#### 动词分类


##### 类别1 实义动词：

- 具有完全的词义，能够独立作为谓语
- 及物动词，如give
- 不及物动词，如rise

##### 类别2 系动词：

- 本身具有词汇意义，但不能单独构成动词词组，必须跟表语一起构成复合谓语
- 如be, become, grow, remain, keep等

##### 类别3 助动词

- **特点：本身没有词汇意义，不能单独作为谓语，只能和及物动词/不及物动词/系动词连用，帮助构成各种时态、语态、否定等。** — do(does/did); have(has/had); be(is/am/are...); will等

###### 时态


###### 一般现在时～常态


###### 用法1：表示永久性的事

- **吸烟喝酒有害健康**

- **这位程序员经常加班。(work overtime)**
  - This programmer often works overtime.
- **她几乎不会购买环保产品（purchase green products）**
  - She seldom/rarely/hardly ever purchases green products.

###### 用法3：表示当下持续的状态

- **收入不均仍是一个问题。（income disparities; remain ）**

### 3.2 状语及状语从句


#### 基本概念

- **状语: 由副词性的词充当，修饰动词、形容词、副词、整个句子等，让别人更清楚事情发生时的“状态”。** — 增加了时间、地点、人物、方式、程度、原因、结果等信息。
- 状语从句：作用同状语，由从属连词引导。

#### 实用状语总结


##### 伴随关系：“随着”


###### 【从属连词as + 完整句子】，主句

- **随着生活成本正在上升，一些人正在更加努力地工作。**
  - As living costs are rising, some people are working harder

###### 【介词with + 名词性成分】，主句

- **随着生活成本的上升，一些人正在更加努力地工作。(the rise in living costs)**
  - With the rise in living costs, some people are working harder.
  - With living rising,.....
- **制造业和建筑业则呈现不同的情况，其比例分别下降至40%和45%。**  _The manufacturing and building sectors；_
  - The manufacturing and building sectors saw a different pattern, with figures dropping to 40% and 45% respectively.

##### 因果关系


###### “因为”


###### 【从属连词Because/since/as + 完整句子】，主句

- **他们选择健康的习惯，因为这些习惯让他们能够去享受生活。(enable sb to do sth)**
  - They choose healthy habits, because these habits enable them to enjoy life.
- **其他人觉得有必要增加公共设施的投资，因为这和人们的生活标准直接相关。(standard of living; have direct relevance to)**
  - Other people consider it necessary to increase the spending on/investment in public facilities, because this has direct relevance to people's standard of living.
- **很多移居者住在贫民窟，因为他们买不起房。**
  - (migrant workers; slums; afford housing)

###### 【介词because of/due to + 名词性成分】，主句

- **因为它们的优点，塑料产品很受消费者欢迎。**
  - Because of their advantages, plastic products have been popular among customers. （介词短语）
- **由于粗心，他被老板解雇了。(carelessness)**
  - Due to carelessness, he was dismissed/fired by his employer/boss. （介词短语）
- **因为他努力工作，所以他取得了一些进步。（分别用because of和because翻译）**
  - Because of his hard work, he achieved some progress.
  - Because he had worked hard, he achieved some progress.

###### “如果”：【从属连词if + 完整句子】，主句

> unless = if not, 可替换使用； 一般来说，主句是将来时，从句是现在时。

- **如果学生只是盲目应用理论，那么他们会发现很多理论无法取得预期效果。(blindly; achieve expected outcomes)**
  - If students apply theories blindly, they will find that many theories may not achieve expected outcomes/serve intended purposes.
- **如果人们要交很高的税，他们就可能没有动力去努力工作。（pay high taxes; incentive动力）**
  - If people need to pay high taxes, they may not have the incentive to work hard.
- **除非你采取必要的预防措施，不然你无法避免事故。**
  - （unless; take precautions）

###### “因此”：【副词therefore/thus + ⚠️什么也不加】，主句

- **在一个更民主的工作环境中，员工们的想法会被尊重。因此，他们会逐渐建立起自信。(a democratic environment; build up confidence)**
  - In a more democratic working environment/workplace, the ideas of staff/employees will be respected. Therefore, they will gradually build up confidence.

##### 转折关系


###### “虽然...但是”


###### 【从属连词Although和though + 完整句子】，主句

> ⚠️although和though本身即可表示让步，如果一定想把“但是”翻译出来，可插入副词nevertheless，但不可用连词but。 🌟 写雅思作文时，如果想要支持的事情也有缺陷，或者想要反对的事情也有优点，那么在开头段中写自己的观点时，经常要用让步状语从句。

- **虽然老一代可以为社会做贡献，但是人口的变化可能导致很多问题。**
  - (the older generation; contribute to society; demographic change)
- **一个没有法律的社会无法维持秩序，尽管过于严格的法律也会威胁社会发展。**
  - (lawless; maintain order; harsh laws)
- **虽然减少碳排放能够最终帮助稳定全球气温，但是这可能对经济发展产生各种负面的影响。**
  - （carbon emissions; stabilize global temperature ）

###### 【介词despite或in spite of + 名词性成分】，主句

- **虽然他很年轻，但他很独立。**
  - Although he is young, he is very independent.
  - Despite his young age, he is very independent.
- **虽然已经花了很多时间学习，她还是挂了这场考试。(countless hours很多时间；fail the exam挂了考试)**
  - Although she had spent countless hours in studying, she still failed the exam.
  - Despite the countless hours spent in studying, she still failed the exam
- **虽然已经收到了大量关于食品质量的投诉，这家餐厅仍然拒绝作出任何改变。（receive numerous complaints收到了大量投诉; make any changes作出任何改变）**
  - Despite numerous complaints about the quality of their food, they refused to make any changes.
  - Although the restaurant had received numerous complaints about the quality of their food, they refused to make any changes.

###### “但是”：【副词however, nevertheless, nonetheless + ⚠️什么也不加】，主句

- **居民想要避免被该病毒感染。但是，如果他们逃离城市，该病毒的蔓延会加快（the spread of the virus病毒的蔓延；flee from逃离）**
  - Residents want to avoid being infected by the virus.However, if they escape/flee(from) the city, the spread of the virus will speed up/accelerate.
  - 或......the virus will spread more quickly.
  - 或....the virus will be spread......
  - 传播迅速的表达：spread fast, like wildfire, quickly, rapidly, soon

###### “另一方面”：【介词短语on the other hand + 什么也不加】，主句

> on the other hand

- **另一方面，法律限制个人自由, 甚至可能阻碍社会发展。**
  - On the other hand, laws inevitably restrict/limit/curtail/inhibit/reduce/threaten personal freedom and may even block/hamper/obstruct/hinder/impede social progress.

###### “相比之下”：【介词短语By contrast + 什么也不加】，主句

- **相比之下，成年的人们不太可能拥有一整周的学习时间。(a whole week's time for study)**
  - By comparison, adults are unlikely to have a whole week' time for study.

##### 并列关系：“此外”


###### 【介词Besides/介词in addition to+ 名词性成分】+ 主句

- **除了温室气体的排放，这些产品还将会污染地下水（greenhouse gas emissions; underground water）**
  - Besides greenhouse gas emissions, these products will also pollute underground water

###### 【副词Besides/介词短语In addition+ 什么也不加】，主句

- **除此之外，激烈的竞争会迫使本地生产商调低价格。(fierce competition; local manufacturer)**
  - Besides/in addition/additionally, fierce competition will force local producers/manufacturers to reduce/cut/lower/bring down prices.

##### 比较关系：“比...更加...”


###### 比较级和最高级

- 不同音节单词的比较级和最高级

###### 改错


###### It is more easy for teenagers to get good grades in one subject they are good at.

- 错误的原因：easy的比较级是easier，
- 提升的句子：It is easier for teenagers to get good grades in one subject they are good at.

- 提升的句子：The government only supports the best students without considering the needs of disadvantaged young people.

- 提升的句子：People can surf the web to read the latest news reports.

###### Some people find it more harder to live their lives without phones.

- 错误的原因：hard比较级就是harder，不需要再加more
- 提升的句子：Some people find it harder to live their lives without phones.

###### 比较结构中经常出现名词重复的情况。可以用that或者those避免重复

- **例句**：例句：His qualification is as good as the qualification of another candidate. 他的资历和另外一个候选人的资历一样好
  - His qualification is as good as that of another candidate. 其中that指代qualification.
- **例句**：例句：The products of this company are better than the products imported from abroad这家公司的产品比国外进口的产品好
  - The products of this company are better than those imported from abroad. 其中those指代products

### 3.3 定语从句


#### 基本概念

- **含义** — 从句充当定语的功能，类似于形容词。
- **先行词** — 被定语从句修饰的词叫先行词（顾名思义，它总是在定语从句左边）
- **关系词** — 引导定语从句的词是关系词（它总在定语从句句首）

##### 如何使用？

- 将名词具体化，发现中文“的”前有动词时

###### 例如：他是个喜欢玩游戏和看小说的男孩儿。

- He is a boy who likes playing games and reading novels.
- boy是先行词
- who是关系词
- who liked playing games and reading novels是定语从句
- 从句还原后为A boy likes playing games and reading novels.

##### 如何检查？

- 写完后把从句还原，也就是把先行词放到从句中，从句应该是一个完整的句子，否则就有问题。
- 比如He was a boy who liked playing games and reading novels.
- 还原后A boy liked playing games and reading novels.

#### 限定性VS非限定性


##### 使用区别


###### 能否缩小先行词范围


###### 限制性定语从句，能够缩小先行词的范围。

- **例句**：例句：The students who watch television frequently may not be able to concentrate on learning tasks.
  - 这里“经常看电视的”就把学生们的范围缩小了，因为并非每个学生都会经常看电视。

###### 非限制性定语从句只是补充信息，并不能够继续缩小先行词的范围。

- **例句**：例句：A smartphone, which allow users to access a wide range of applications and services, is very common now.
  - 这里的“使得用户能使用广泛的应用和服务”，并不能继续缩小智能手机的范围，因为这本就是“智能手机”这个词所蕴含的内容。

###### 能否修饰一整句话

- 只有非限制性定语从句才能修饰整句话

##### 书写注意

- 非限制性定语从句要加逗号
- 非限定性定语从句不用that引导
- **媒体已经将我们的注意力吸引到奥林匹克运动会上，而这是一个对运动员很重要的事件。(The media; draw one's attention to sth; the Olympics; a significant event for athletes)**
  - The media have/has drawn our attention to Olympics, which is a significant event for athletes.
- **政府鼓励人们勤奋工作，这样能够让经济不断发展。（be able to; keep the economy running）**
  - The government encourages people to work hard, which is able to keep the economy running.

#### 定从的省略

> 不建议自己写定语从句时省略，只需看得懂别人写的省略句子。


##### 关系词作宾语时

- **例句**：例句：The colleague (that) I mentioned has quitted his job.（关系词that可保留也可省略）

###### 找出下列句中的定语从句


###### The coal we burn today was once living plants.

- The coal (which) we burn today was once living plants.

###### The technicians we met yesterday had worked out a new plan.

- The technicians (whom) we met yesterday had worked out a new plan.

##### 关系词作主语时

- **例句**：例句：University students seek to acquire skills (which are) essential to their future careers.
- **例句**：例句：The information (which was) provided by the government was accurate.

###### 找出下列句中的定语从句


###### There are two large rooms available for hire.

- = There are two large rooms which are available for hire.
- 省略关系词which和系动词are

###### These students were reading materials given by teachers

- = These students were reading materials which were given by teachers
- 省略关系词which和助动词were

###### The man driving the bus was drunk.

- = The man who was driving the bus was drunk.
- 省略关系词who和助动词was，

###### They live in a room facing the south

- = They live in a room which faces the south
- 省略关系词which, 将原本动词写成分词形式

### 3.4 名词性从句


#### 基本概念

- **含义** — 在主句中起名词作用的句子。
- **类型：主语、宾语、表语和同位语从句** — 从句充当主句什么成分，就是什么从句（如出现在主语位置，就叫主语从句）

##### 何时使用？

- 想用一句话充当名词时

###### 例如：我/相信/人口正在变老

- I believe (that) the population is growing older.宾语从句

###### The fact/ is / that the population is growing older.

#### That引导的名词性从句


##### that引导的宾语从句

> 句子结构必须完整，且不可以倒装（也就是属于某种基本句型）

- **例句**：例句：I believe that education can have a huge impact on one's career.
- **我认为徒刑会对年轻人造成伤害，因为犯罪记录会给毁掉他们的职业生涯（prison sentences; cause harm to; criminal records犯罪记录; ruin their careers）**
  - I think that prison sentences will inflict/cause harm to young people, because criminal records will ruin their careers.
  - I think that imposing sentences/punishment.....

##### that引导的主语从句

> 主句的主语一般用形式主语it，而真正的主语从句放在后面

- **例句**：例句：It is obvious that poverty can threaten social stability. 贫穷显然会威胁到社会的稳定。
- **例句**：例句：It is widely believed that poverty can threaten social stability. 大家都相信贫困会威胁到社会的稳定。
- **（有人）主张严苛的处罚能够威慑犯罪。（argue; a harsh penalty; deter crimes）**
  - It is argued that a harsh penalty can deter crimes.

##### that引导的表语从句

> that前面有个is，而从句充当主语的表语

- **例句**：例句：The main problem of eating fast food every day is that it is harmful to our health.
- **制定规则的一个优点是，规则能够帮助孩子塑造良好行为。（Imposing rules; develop good behavior patterns）**
  - One benefit of imposing rules is that rules can help children develop good behavior patterns.

##### that引导的同位语从句

> 出现在一个名词后面，同时句子结构完整（不同于that引导的定从）

- **例句**：例句：We have heard the news that he is about to graduate from university.（同位语类似定语，但与其修饰的成分在语法上处于同等地位，起相同的句法作用。）
- **尽管很多电视节目都有教育价值，我不赞同学生应多看电视这一看法。（have educational value; increase screen time）**
  - Although many television programs have educational value, I do not agree with the idea that students should increase their screen time.
- **勤奋的工作会得到回报这一信念是很普遍的(pay off得到回报；belief信念; widespread普遍的)**
  - The belief that hard work pays off is widespread.

##### that有时可能省略

> 不建议自己写名词性从句时省略，只需看得懂别人写的省略句子。

- 判断以下句子中that原本的位置

###### It is certain he doesn't object.

> 他肯定不会反对。

- It is certain(that) he doesn't object.

###### I expect he will arrive at six.

> 我预计他会6点钟到。

- I expect (that) he will arrive at six.

###### He supposed it was too late to change his mind.

> 他认为已经来不及改变主意了。

- He supposed (that) it was too late to change his mind.

###### They consider the critical period is not over.

> 他们认为危急时刻并未过去

- They consider (that) the critical period is not over.

#### Whether引导的名词性从句


##### 用法类似that，区别在于

- that表示对一个确定事情的陈述
- whether表示对一个不确定事情的描述

##### whether引导的主语从句

- **例句**：例句：年轻人是否可以提高独立性/是/一个主要的问题

- **这个宝藏是否存在是一个谜。**
  - It is a mystery whether the treasure still exists (or not)
  - whether the treasure still exists (or not) is a mystery.

##### whether引导的宾语从句

- **例句**：例句：父母不知道年轻人是否可以通过海外留学去提高独立性（study abroad; develop independence）

- **来自农村的移居者不知道他们能否在城市找到工作。(Migrants from rural areas）**
  - Migrants from rural areas do not know whether they can find employment in cities.

##### whether引导的同位语从句

- **例句**：例句：父母已经提出的问题是：年轻人能否通过去海外留学提高独立性。
  - Parents have raised the question whether young people can develop independence by studying abroad.

  - People often debate whether studying in a university is the best approach to achieving success.

###### 改错：Some teachers argued that whether students should do extra homework.

- 中文是：一些老师认为学生应该做额外作业，所以去掉whether，因为that和whether重复了：Some teachers argued that students should do extra homework.
- 中文是：一些老师讨论了学生是否应该做额外作业，那么去掉that，同时argued要改成debated/discussed。因为argue这里不是争论/争吵，而是认为/主张：Some teachers debated whether students should do extra homework.

  - This leader showed employees how they could gain a sense of progress.

### 3.6 复杂句


#### 含义：包含两个或者更多从句和并列结构的句子

- **状语一般是副词性质（副词、介宾短语、分词），但是出现了从句（状语从句）** — 如果复杂句中出现状语从句，主句和从句一般都是完整句子。
- **定语一般是形容词性质（副词、介宾短语、分词），但是出现了从句（定语从句）** — 先行词放在从句里必定可以还原，而还原后的句子也必定完整。
- **主语、宾语、同位语、表语一般都是名词性质，但是出现了从句（名词性从句）** — 如果是that/when/where/why引导的名词性从句，则从句是完整的。

### 4.2 ❤️ 核心概念


#### 句子成分

- **主语** — 动作的发出者。
- **谓语** — 动作本身。
- **宾语** — 动作的接受者。
- **宾语补足语** — 某些及物动词带了直接宾语后，意义仍不完整，需要一些词或词组（即宾语补足语）补充说明宾语的动作或状态。
- **表语** — 也称主语补足语，用于说明主语的身份、性质、品性、特征和状态。
- **定语** — 由形容词性的词充当，修饰名词性的成分。
- **状语** — 由副词性的词充当，修饰动词、形容词、副词、整个句子等。
- **同位语** — 类似定语，但与其修饰的成分在语法上处于同等地位，起相同的句法作用。

#### 单词词性

> 不同词性的单词可能充当同一个句子成分。

- **及物动词** — 后面可直接跟宾语的动词，可单独构成谓语。
- **不及物动词** — 后面不可直接跟宾语的动词，可单独构成谓语。
- **系动词** — 连接主语和表语的动词，不能单独作为谓语，与表语一起构成复合谓语。
- **情态动词** — 本身具有词义，但不能单独作为谓语，只能和动词原形连用构成复合谓语。 用法1.表示对现在/过去/将来事实的推测 用法2.表示能力、责任、批评、建议、必要、允许、禁止、态度、习惯等意念。
- **助动词** — 本身没有词汇意义，不能单独作为谓语，只能和及物动词/不及物动词/系动词连用，帮助构成各种时态、语态、否定等。

##### be动词


###### 用法1: 作为助动词

- **构成进行时态** — He is playing a game.
- **构成被动语态** — He was kissed by her.
- **用法2: 作为系动词：主系表句型的一部分** — He is a student. He is happy.

### 4.3 🔧 检查句子


#### 句型错误

- 成分残缺或多余
- ❌ 句子 ：There are many people prefer to drive cars.
- 分析：出现了双谓语：are和prefer，需将后者写进从句，确保主句只有一个谓语动词
- ✅ 句子：There are many people who prefer to drive cars.

#### 谓语错误


##### 主谓不一致

- ❌ 句子：There is many people who prefer to drive cars.
- 分析：主语是many people，和is不一致
- ✅ 句子：There are many people who prefer to drive cars.

##### 时态不合适

- 1⃣️ 句子：Pollution became a serious problem.
- 分析：对比：如果用became（过去时），表示污染过去是个问题，暗示现在不是了；
- 2⃣️ 句子：Pollution has become a serious problem.
- 分析：用has become(现在完成时)，表示污染从过去的某个时间点开始就是个问题，直到现在

##### 多写或漏写助动词等

- ❌ 句子：The shortage of water is cannot attract worldwide attention.
- 分析：is 是构成进行时态或被动语态的助动词或构成主系表句型的系动词，在这里是多余的。
- ✅ 句子：The shortage of water is attracting worldwide attention.

#### 词性错误

- ❌ 句子：Play football can improve health.
- 分析：动词原型不能作主语，需写成名词性的形式，即动名词
- ✅ 句子：Playing football can improve health.

#### 名词单复数

- ❌ 句子：Foreign visitors find it hard to communicate with the staffs of local hotels.
- 分析：staff本身已经是复数，不能加s
- ✅ 句子：Foreign visitors find it hard to communicate with the staff of local hotels.

- https://www.collinsdictionary.com/zh/
- https://dictionary.cambridge.org
- https://www.ldoceonline.com

#### 如果不确定单词之间的固定搭配，查牛津搭配词典

- https://ozdic.com

#### 如果词伙较长，搭配词典里找不到，可使用语料库

- **https://www.fraze.it** — 查例句
- **https://www.linggle.com** — 查使用频率
