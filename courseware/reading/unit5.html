2A · Lead-in · b) Discuss and Reveal
讨论与揭秘 Discuss and Reveal
  1. Be honest: Which picture looks like your actual weekend when nobody is watching? Which picture do you usually post on your social media?
  2. How do you feel when you see people constantly posting "Picture A" lifestyles online? Does it motivate you, or make you feel exhausted?
  3. In 2022, Oxford University Press announced their Word of the Year: Goblin Mode. It perfectly describes "Picture B". Why do you think such a lazy, messy lifestyle became the most popular word in the world after the pandemic?
【教学指引 — 2A(b) Discuss and Reveal 思路参考】Q1: "Which picture looks like your actual weekend? Which do you post?"引导方向：多数学生实际生活更像 Picture B（懒散/休息），但社交媒体上更倾向于展示 Picture A（精致/高效）。引出"社交媒体与现实差距"这一主题——这正是 Goblin Mode 文章的核心背景。Q2: "How do you feel when you see people posting Picture A lifestyles?"引导方向：学生可能会说 motivated（受激励）、exhausted（感到疲惫）、inadequate（觉得自己不够好）。关键追问：为什么看到别人的完美生活会让你觉得累？——引向 performative culture（表演式文化）和 social pressure（社交压力）的讨论。Q3: "Why did Goblin Mode become the most popular word after the pandemic?"引导方向：疫情让人们厌倦了表演——封闭期间，没人能看到你的精致生活，维持完美形象失去了意义。核心词：reject social norms（拒绝社会规范）、unapologetically lazy（理直气壮地懒）、authenticity over performance（真实胜过表演）。这也呼应 Paragraph 5 的核心论点。教学提示：讨论时间控制在 5-8 分钟，不要让讨论变成闲聊——每轮讨论后用 Speaking Help 句型框架引导总结，自然过渡到 Pre-teach Vocabulary。

At the start of the Covid pandemic, as people were finding themselves locked in their houses for weeks at a time, a certain breed of holier-than-thou go-getter appeared who took their physical isolation as an opportunity to very publicly 'better' themselves in all manner of ways. Q35 — 词义推断 Inference（段落 1）题目：A 'holier-than-thou go-getter' (line 2) is someone who is …答案：B. self-righteous and ambitious解析：holier-than-thou = 自以为高人一等的（与 self-righteous "自以为是的" 同义）；go-getter = 敢拼敢闯的人（与 ambitious "雄心勃勃的" 同义）。定位 §1："a certain breed of holier-than-thou go-getter appeared who took their physical isolation as an opportunity to very publicly 'better' themselves" → "very publicly" 与 "better" 凸显了炫耀+自以为是的意味。干扰项分析：A (religious) 错——"holier-than-thou" 在此不是宗教含义，而是比喻"自命清高"；C (reliable/helpful) 和 D (confident/assertive) 偏离了关键的"自以为是"意味。🔑 生词推断三步法：(1) 拆词——holier-than-thou = holier + than + thou（我比你更神圣→自命清高）；go-getter = go + get + er（去拿的人→进取者）；(2) 看上下文态度——"nauseating displays"（令人作呕的炫耀）暗示作者对此类人的负面态度；(3) 排除不合负面/自以为是意味的选项。

3 · Key Reading Skills
3A 题型一：Summary Completion（填空/完形摘要）

📖 Summary Completion 技能

Summary Completion（填空/完形摘要）是一种精准定位与语义对齐的过程。Summary 中的每一个空格，都能在原文中找到对应的意思，你的工作是在原文与 summary 之间建立连接，再将原文的表达适配成符合 summary 语法结构的形式。

在做这类题目时，你需要关注以下三点：

① 这个空格在 summary 的句子中需要什么词性？（名词、形容词、副词？）

② 原文中哪个位置的哪个词或短语，与 summary 这个位置的意思对应？

③ 填入后，语法是否通顺？与 summary 整体语气是否一致？

你刚才完成的 Q46 正是考查这种能力。

Summary Completion 涉及以下考试题型：

题型考点典型问法
Summary Completion理解原文内容并将其转化为符合 summary 语法结构的词汇Complete the summary using words/phrases from the passage. / Complete the summary based on information in paragraphs X.

46 Based on the information in paragraphs 9 and 10, complete the summary by writing a word or phrase taken from these paragraphs in each blank below. Your answers must be grammatically correct. (5 marks)

The writer thinks it is (i) ____________________ that the concept of goblin mode is here to stay, because he or she has already taken to this (ii) ____________________ and done so (iii) ____________________________, without even realizing it. 1. 做 Q46 时，怎么判断是在空格中写文章的原词，还是改词？

2. 如何判断摘要中的内容会出现在文章中的哪一段呢？

3. 对比原文定位段落/句子的时候，文章和题目中的哪些信息容易替换？

4. 空格中应该填写什么词性，什么语义的词？

3A · Summary Completion 解题框架
📖 Summary Completion 解题框架

题目特点：

  • 答案词是否可改写，看题目指示：含 "using words taken from paragraph X" / "with words from paragraphs X" → 答案词必须直接来自原文
  • 无以上指示，尤其附加了 "make sure your answers are grammatically correct" 时 → 答案词可以不用照搬原文
  • 题干句通常使用了改写/同义替换（paraphrasing）
  • 信息顺序可能与原文不同
  • 答案必须语法正确（注意时态、单复数），拼写必须完全正确

解题步骤：

Step 1

阅读题目指示

确认段落范围、判断答案类型（Type A / Type B）、注意填写词数要求。

Step 2

阅读题目，确定定位信息

理解空格所在句子的整体意思（暂时忽略空格）；找出空格附近未被替换的词——这些词往往直接出现在原文对应位置附近，是最可靠的锚点；以这些词扫读原文，锁定对应位置。

Step 3

预判空格信息

根据空格在句中的位置，预判所需词性、词义范围及单复数形式。

Step 4

对比 summary 与原文，确认语义对齐，确定答案

将空格句与原文对应句并排，确认语义是否对齐。若空格附近无明显定位词：先忽略所有词汇，只问自己：「这句话在说什么事？」——然后去原文找说同一件事的句子。

类型处理方式
Type A (taken from)直接提取原文词汇，只调整语法形式
Type B (fit taken from)先理解原文的概念，再用符合空格词性的词汇重新表达；重点是语义准确，而非词汇相同
Step 5

填入答案，检查语法与拼写

重读整句话，确保语法通顺；仔细检查拼写，每个字母都不能错。

3A 题型二：Inference of Authorial Purpose（作者写作意图推断）

🎯 Authorial Purpose 技能

文章中每一个选择——用哪个例子、在哪里引用数据、为何提到某个对比——都不是偶然的。Authorial Purpose 题要求你读懂作者为什么在这里放这个内容，而不只是他说了什么。

在处理这类题目时，你需要问自己三个问题：

① 作者在这里放了什么——一个例子、一段引言、一个对比，还是一个数据？

② 这个内容在文章的论证中起什么作用——是支撑、举例、反驳，还是强调？

③ 如果去掉这个内容，文章的论点会少了什么？

你刚才完成的 Q49 正是考查这种能力。

Authorial Purpose 涉及以下考试题型：

题型考点典型问法
Inference of Purpose理解作者选用特定例子、引言或内容的写作意图Why does the writer use/include '…'? / Why does the writer mention X in paragraph Y?

49 Why does the writer use some former 'that girl' followers and a middle-aged writer as examples in paragraph 12?

回顾你在相关题目作答的过程，思考以下问题：

1. 作者在第 12 段选用了两个最然不同的人作为例子——"that girl" followers 和一个中年作家。这两个人有什么共同点？又有什么不同之处？

2. 作者为什么不只举一个例子，而要举两个背景完全不同的人？这个选择传递了什么信息？

3. 现在试着用一句话写出：这两个例子合在一起，想让读者相信什么？（提示：不要描述例子本身，而是说它们共同证明了什么论点。）

3A · Authorial Purpose 解题框架
🎯 Inference of Authorial Purpose 解题框架
Step 1

识别作者放了什么内容

确认题目指向的是例子、数据、引言、对比，还是特定的句子结构。

第二步

问「这个内容在做什么工作」

内容类型常见功能英文表达（答卷参考）
具体例子使抽象论点具体化；扩大论点的适用范围to illustrate / to exemplify / to show that…
数据 / 统计为主张提供客观证据to support the claim with evidence / to demonstrate that…
引言 / 他人观点支持或反驳自身立场；增加权威性to reinforce the point / to counter the view that…
对比突出差异；强调某一方的特点to highlight / to contrast X with Y / to emphasize that…
反例预判读者质疑；增强论证全面性to acknowledge / to address the counterargument that…
第二步半

从内容跳到论点

找到功能之后，问自己：这个内容想让读者相信什么？如果作者只能说一句话，他会说什么？——这句话，才是 "to show that" 后面的内容。

Step 3

用「to + 动词」句式组织答案

作答时以 to do 开头，直接点明写作目的，而非描述内容本身。

✗ 错误示范：The writer uses a "that girl" follower and a middle-aged writer.（只描述内容，没有说明目的）

✓ 正确示范：To show that goblin mode applies to people of all backgrounds — not just a specific group.（以 to do 开头，直接点明写作意图）

💡 检验：你 "to show that" 后面写的，是在描述「作者用了什么」，还是在说明「作者想证明什么」？如果是前者，需要再往上抽象一层。

3C 题型三：Text Type（文体识别）

📚 Text Type 技能

Text Type 题要求你判断整篇文章属于哪种文体。不同文体有不同的写作目的、语气和结构特征——识别文体，本质上是理解「这篇文章是为谁写的、为什么写」。

常见文体及核心特征：

文体核心特征
Column个人声音强烈、带有明确立场与幽默感，定期刊登于报纸/杂志
Feature Article信息性为主，较为平衡客观，面向普通读者
Essay论点结构严谨，语气较学术，个人色彩相对较弱
Review评估某件事物（书、电影、产品），有明确评价性结论

3C · Text Type 解题方法
📚 Text Type 解题方法
Step 1

判断是否有强烈的个人声音

问自己：文章中有没有第一人称、直接表达意见、幽默或讽刺、个人经历？如果有，说明作者「声音」鲜明，倾向 Column 或 Letter to the editor；如果主要是客观叙述，倾向 Feature Article or Report 类。

Step 2

判断写作目的

是为了告知、说服、评论，还是分享个人观点？

Step 3

逐一排除选项

对照各文体特征，排除不符合的，保留最吻合的。

Text 4: The year of the goblins

§1
At the start of the Covid pandemic, as people were finding themselves locked in their houses for weeks at a time, a certain breed of holier-than-thou go-getter appeared who took their physical isolation as an opportunity to very publicly 'better' themselves in all manner of ways. They would take up yoga, for example, or sourdough baking, or gardening, or embroidery—all while gleefully rubbing their achievements in everyone else's face on social media.
§2
Due to the relentless compulsion within modern society to pigeonhole and bestow catchy terms onto every mundane aspect of human behaviour, these nauseating displays of in-your-face domesticity very quickly started to be categorized as examples of 'that girl' or 'cottagecore' aesthetics (this latter term also appeasing the equally unyielding compulsion these days to give every little change in fashion or lifestyle trends the name whatever-core).
§3
After almost three years of Covid, however, it appears that some of this egregious pep has abated. For evidence, look no further than the announcement this week that the Oxford University Press Word of the Year for 2022 is 'goblin mode'. The venerable publishing house defines the word (well, the words, really, or perhaps the phrase—but who am I to quibble) as 'a type of behaviour which is unapologetically self-indulgent, lazy, slovenly, or greedy, typically in a way that rejects social norms or expectations'.
§4
Oh, dear. 'Self-indulgent, lazy, slovenly, or greedy'? That sounds like perfectly dreadful behaviour, doesn't it? Digging a little deeper, though, we find this example from Dave McNamee, whose tweets on goblin mode helped to popularize the concept in early 2022:
§5
'Goblin mode is like when you wake up at 2 a.m. and shuffle into the kitchen wearing nothing but a long T-shirt to make a weird snack, like melted cheese on saltines. It's about a complete lack of aesthetic. Because why would a goblin care what they look like? Why would a goblin care about presentation?'
§6
Ah, I see. When we get right down to it, then, 'going goblin mode' simply means 'occasionally being a bit of a slob in the privacy of your own home'. It's not hard to see why that would be an appealing concept at this juncture in history. Nor is there any question that the term itself has found considerable popularity.
§7
OUP made an important change to the selection process for this year's word, in that they opened it up to the public. 'Goblin mode' very appropriately went goblin mode on the opposition, soundly beating the other two contenders, 'metaverse' and '#IStandWith', into proverbial pulps by garnering a whopping 93 per cent of the more than 300,000 total votes cast.
§8
'Given the year we've just experienced,' goblin mode" resonates with all of us who are feeling a little overwhelmed at this point,' explained Casper Grathwohl, President of Oxford Languages. 'It's a relief to acknowledge that we're not always the idealized, curated selves that we're encouraged to present on our Instagram and TikTok feeds. […] People are embracing their inner goblin, and voters choosing 'goblin mode' as the Word of the Year tells us the concept is likely here to stay.'
§9
Well, that's excellent news, Casper my boy. Because unlike 'that girl' and 'cottagecore', 'goblin mode' is one stupidly named fad I can get behind. Indeed, I seem to have perfected the artform already, without even realizing that what I was doing was so on trend. Not ten minutes before I sat down to write this piece, I resorted—without a second thought—to putting my dirty dishes in the bathroom because I'd run out of space in the sink. And trust me, you don't even want to know what subconsciously goblin-inspired attire I was (and still am) wearing.
§10
Not all people are as inherently and effortlessly with it as I am, however. There is a powerfully stubborn nature to those 'social norms' and 'expectations' referenced in the OUP definition, and people are understandably loath to break them. This, I'll grant you, is not an altogether bad thing. I'm quite happy, for example, that my doctor isn't so unburdened by societal conventions that he feels comfortable showing up to his clinic dressed only in his underwear. But there is something borderline pathological about our having become so socialized that we don't even feel free to 'misbehave' when we're completely alone and nobody is watching.
§11
Hopefully, with the 'word' 'goblin mode' having been officially sanctioned and promoted by no less an authority than Oxford University Press, the behaviour it denotes will become more widely accepted by extension, and we'll see the normalization of something that should really have been considered normal to begin with: the urge to set those annoying social norms and expectations aside every once in a while and embrace your worse nature.
§12
The way I see it, that urge should be encouraged whenever possible, whether the result is some former 'that girl' followers tweeting about going goblin mode after workout or a middle-aged writer stepping over grease-encrusted plates while wearing only an ancient dressing-gown with holes in all the wrong places. Because no matter how hideous they may be, the world certainly needs more goblins—at least on a part-time basis.

Text 4: Shedding light on a city's blight

§1
There is nothing quite as awe-inspiring as looking up at a starry sky. For centuries, stargazers have been captivated by the billions of stars and planets that brighten our nights. But if staring at the stars is something you really like to do, Hong Kong might not be the right place for you. It is getting harder and harder to see the stars from the city. The difficulty stems from Hong Kong's enormous levels of light pollution, which lately have been going from bad to worse. In fact, Hong Kong now has the worst light pollution on earth.
§2
This unfortunate distinction has been assigned to the city as the result of a three-year study. The study, the largest and most comprehensive of its kind, was conducted by a team of physicists from the University of Hong Kong, led by Dr Jason Pun. The team took millions of sky brightness measurements, compiling the largest ever data set on light pollution. Based on the data, the researchers were able to determine that the night sky in Hong Kong is over 1000 times brighter than a sky without any artificial light. There is no other place in the world where the researchers were able to record such high numbers. 'In major European cities, such as Vienna or Berlin, the night skies are only about 100 to 200 times brighter than a normal sky,' Pun says.
§3
Unsurprisingly, light pollution affects different areas with varying degrees of severity. The team found the very worst level of light pollution directly above the Space Museum in Tsim Sha Tsui. 'Hong Kong is an extremely densely populated place, which is why the city has such a severe problem,' Pun says. However, Pun and his team also examined the city's surrounding suburbs and rural areas and found that elevated levels of light pollution exist even in these less populated regions. This discovery is very telling. It means that the problem of light pollution is no longer contained to the main commercial hub of Hong Kong. More and more people are being affected.
§4
In recent years, there has been a sharp rise in the number of complaints about light pollution. Just a few years ago, the Environment Bureau received only a handful of complaints. This year, however, the number of complaints they have received has skyrocketed into the hundreds. People are starting to be fed up with the amount of light flooding into their homes at night. In an effort to address this problem, the Environment Bureau has put forward a set of guidelines for the use of exterior lighting. However, these guidelines are simply suggestions. There are currently no laws in place limiting light usage. Other countries, including South Korea and the United Kingdom, have started taking steps to combat their light pollution problems by implementing restrictions on the unnecessary use of light. Many environmentalists have been calling for similar laws to be passed in Hong Kong, especially in the wake of this recent study.
§5
Of course, the idea of introducing such laws is not universally welcomed. Retailers, accustomed to their neon signs attracting attention twenty-four hours a day, are not exactly eager to see their bright lights dimmed. There is also the tourism industry, which often markets the thousands of twinkling lights as a top selling point of the city. But it is not just commercial operators that are reluctant to see light pollution laws implemented. Some residents view the lights as a wonderful feature of the city, a feature they are not willing to lose. Local resident Tammy Cheung says, 'I love our lights. I think they make the city unique.'
§6
Unfortunately, this unique feature of the city is coming at a steep cost. Light pollution has been linked to a wide range of negative health effects, most obvious of which is the disruption of sleep patterns and biological rhythms. Humans have evolved daily biological rhythms based on the light cycle of a day. During the daytime hours, when the sun is up, we tend to be awake and engaged in activity. During the night-time hours, when the sun has set, we tend to be asleep. Because artificial light disrupts this natural light cycle, it can wreak havoc on our daily biological rhythms. Gina Yu, a prominent sleep expert, explains the effect of bright lights shining into our bedrooms: 'Our bodies interpret light as a signal that it's time to be awake. So when the light signal comes in the middle of the night, just as you're trying to get some much-needed rest, it can be difficult for your body to relax enough for you to fall asleep. You're competing against millions of years of evolution.' It is not all bad news, though. As Yu explains: 'Artificial light is great if you really love nightlife. All the lights in this city will help you stay awake enough to enjoy the evening entertainment.' Eventually, however, it is important to get a sufficient amount of sleep, and this is not always easy. Sleep-related problems, such as insomnia, have been widely reported in areas where light pollution is severe. The lack of sleep can, in turn, lead to many kinds of health issues, from a poor attention span and grogginess to more serious illnesses.
§7
The health effects of light pollution are not limited to humans. The problem is having an effect on local wildlife as well. Nocturnal animals can easily become confused by the absence of truly dark nights. They, like humans, rely on light signals to set their behaviours. Certain nocturnal birds are particularly sensitive to light pollution for yet another reason. They depend in part on the stars to assist in navigating their migrations. When the stars become obscured by large amounts of light pollution, the birds can become disoriented and lose their way. Another species that seems to have been especially affected by light pollution is the firefly. Fireflies are insects that use light to communicate with one another. Amid excessive artificial light, it is harder for them to spot each other's signals. In recent years, their populations have suffered an abrupt decline. Scientists are speculating that light pollution is responsible for the drop.
§8
It is clear that light pollution is having a strong impact on the city and surrounding area. Possible solutions to the problem must be explored in the years to come. Given the fact that there are so many different opinions on the matter, these solutions will certainly be the subject of much debate. Dr Pun suggests that any solutions will require a joint effort between the government, the business sector and the community. Because light pollution is a problem that affects everyone, everyone will have to play a part in its solution.
