Text 1 — The Green Neighborhood

¶1
The neighborhood started a recycling program last year. Many volunteers joined to help clean the streets.
¶2
A local supermarket supported the plan by giving discounts to people who recycled.
中文翻译

¶1 这个社区去年启动了一个回收计划。许多志愿者加入帮忙清理街道。

¶2 一家本地超市通过给回收的人打折来支持这个计划。
