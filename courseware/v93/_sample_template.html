🔑 名词 Nouns

  • abandon — 放弃；抛弃
  • accuracy — 准确性；精确
  • ambiguity — 含糊；歧义

🔑 动词 Verbs

  • assess — 评估；评定
  • conclude — 得出结论
  • negotiate — 协商；谈判

记忆法：abandon = a + band + on → 把乐队丢下自己走了 → 抛弃。用词根联想比死记更牢。

[1] The researcher abandoned the traditional method after noticing its limitations.

[2] A new approach was adopted to improve accuracy and reduce ambiguity in the results.

中文翻译：研究员在注意到传统方法的局限后，放弃了它。
中文翻译：为提高准确性、减少结果中的歧义，采用了新方法。
According to the passage, why did the researcher abandon the method?

定位词：abandon + limitations。题干问 why，回原文找原因从句。
